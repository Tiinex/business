# Public Trust, Contribution And Governance Surface

## Objective

Provide a concise public trust surface that lets contributors, users, and potential supporters understand Tiinex identity, licensing, contribution boundaries, security/contact paths, governance, support, and funding without reading internal history.

## Done Criteria

- Public project surfaces clearly distinguish Tiinex identity, current grounding, historical material, and experimental work.
- Organization-level contribution, security, support, governance, and funding guidance can be projected without duplicating hidden competing authority.
- Public files point back to durable Tiinex authority where deeper provenance matters.
- The surface remains credible for a one-human open-source project using AI roles without representing AI as legal persons or employees.

## Scope

- Do not create marketing claims unsupported by current artifacts.
- Keep public projection shorter than internal provenance.
- Do not make GitHub presentation files the sole semantic authority for Business decisions.

## Dependencies

- Business organization/role/initiative artifacts.
- Current Tiinex .github orientation material and Apache-2.0 licensing.
