# Blind Fresh-Anchor Thin-Lineage Validation

## Objective

Prove whether a genuinely fresh Anchor can recover enough operating model from thin qualified Tiinex authority to form and execute a sound bounded orchestration plan without Sigma reconstructing the organization or this Master Anchor supplying hidden chat context.

## Test Design Boundary

The validation subject must not receive the rich current Master conversation, emergency overlay, retrospective findings, or a hand-authored explanation of the expected grounding behaviors before its first execution. The test carrier should expose only the minimum qualified material necessary for a legitimate bounded task and its authority.

The controlling Anchor may use current Business/Docs/Core state to design and audit the test, but the subject carrier must remain intentionally sparse enough to distinguish Tiinex/Role/process grounding from inherited parent-history privilege.

## Required Behaviors To Observe, Not Coach

Do not enumerate these as instructions to the test subject. The controlling Anchor evaluates whether the fresh subject naturally:

- resolves its own Role and explicit holder binding;
- distinguishes route action readiness from broader orchestration sufficiency;
- derives participant/capability relevance only from qualified authority;
- discovers any material process obligations through forward controlling authority rather than inventory/reverse scanning;
- identifies source sufficiency and emits an exact blocker instead of opportunistic external recovery when authority is missing;
- forms a stable bounded frontier and uses specialist delegation when appropriate;
- respects Business/human/repository boundaries;
- treats carrier progress and recovery as stewardship rather than arbitrary naming;
- leaves a successor state that another fresh Anchor can recover without Sigma reconstruction.

## Variants

Start with one deliberately thin but legitimate carrier. If that passes, later variants may test:

- complete Business available;
- Business absent with qualified `.cache.zip`/bounded material;
- intentionally unavailable authoritative source that should produce an exact blocker.

Do not conflate the first validation with production Site/Playthings orchestration.

## Diagnostic Procedure

1. Spawn a **fresh Anchor** project/chat.
2. Provide only the qualified test Handoff package and its cold-start routing text.
3. Let the fresh Anchor perform its first bounded run without corrective coaching.
4. Before any second assignment or correction, collect the standard non-leading first-run retrospective.
5. Compare received qualified context, the subject's pre-retrospective behavior/output, and its self-reported interpretation.
6. Classify gaps as semantic, Role/process, Handoff/carriage, Tooling/projection, recovery, or execution defects.
7. Persist accepted fixes into Tiinex authority/tooling; do not solve the test by enlarging chat-only explanation.

## Done Criteria

- One fresh Anchor completes a first thin-lineage run with no Sigma grounding reconstruction before the retrospective.
- A non-leading retrospective is captured before any corrective second assignment.
- The controlling Anchor can identify which expected operating concepts were naturally recovered, unresolved, or missing.
- Any material gap has an explicit owner and durable remediation path.
- A verified Full Recovery checkpoint exists before production Site/Playthings sub-Anchor work is opened.

## Scope

Current Carrier Major 001 thin-lineage grounding front only. This Task validates grounding; it does not itself implement Site/Playthings features or authorize a production secondary Anchor.

## Dependencies

- accepted Axiom process-applicability semantic disposition;
- accepted Loom grounding-introspection/authoring Core return;
- process-aware Anchor Role continuation;
- second-specialist reconciliation Decision;
- latest verified integrated Full Recovery carrier.

## Boundaries

- No Sigma coaching before the first-run retrospective.
- No use of the emergency re-grounding markdown until after the natural-grounding snapshot if a diagnostic comparison is desired.
- No remote/source archaeology to make the subject appear successful.
- Failure is valid evidence and should be preserved rather than manually repaired inside the test run.
