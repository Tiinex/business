# Tiinex Roadmap

## Roadmap Principle

This roadmap answers **what should happen next and in what order**. It is phase-gated rather than date-driven because Business does not yet have evidence for reliable multi-month delivery estimates, staffing capacity, or funded runway.

The roadmap owns **phase membership, ordering, phase purpose, and phase exit intent**. It does **not** own or copy Epic/Task status, owner, blocker, progress percentage, detailed next step, or acceptance evidence. Those remain authoritative in the linked work artifacts.

Exact external deadlines remain exact where they already exist — for example the current public challenge deadline. New calendar promises should be introduced through explicit schedule/milestone artifacts when the commitment and capacity are real.

Funding can accelerate or stabilize delivery, but it does not lower the acceptance criteria for a phase.

For a current roll-up of this roadmap, use [Roadmap Follow](001-5-1-roadmap-follow.trace.md). The Follow resolves linked work state from its owning artifacts rather than storing a second status table here.

## Position Before The Current Phase — Demonstrated Foundation Material

Tiinex enters this roadmap with meaningful work already completed or demonstrated:

- a substantial public artifact/provenance and schema model;
- working portable Tooling and real recovery/cold-start experience;
- public repositories and a current Site implementation direction;
- an earlier Viewer PoC demonstrating useful human navigation and provenance comprehension patterns;
- current Business organization, Roles, Initiatives, financing boundaries, and Foundation governance;
- real public contribution/bounty activity that creates an external obligation rather than a hypothetical governance exercise.

This is why Foundation Readiness is a consolidation phase, not “start building Tiinex.”

## Phase 2 — Qualified Public Baseline And Development Thaw — Next

**Starts when:** Foundation Readiness is accepted.

**Outcome:** normal product development resumes from a public baseline that a contributor or user can understand and verify without hidden project history.

**Existing organizational anchors:**

- [Core](001-1-core-project.trace.md)
- [Tooling](001-2-tooling-project.trace.md)
- [Viewer](001-3-viewer-project.trace.md)
- [Artifact Lineage And Provenance Comprehension](001-3-2-artifact-lineage-provenance-comprehension-task.trace.md)

These are current authority anchors, not a claim that all Phase 2 execution Epics have already been materialized. New technical work packages should be created in their natural repositories only when a real bounded unit of work exists, with relations/Parent paths preserving the organizational reason upward.

**Phase intent:** resolve the qualified public Viewer/reference baseline, re-open ordinary development, establish a bounded contributor path, and begin exercising financial provenance on real events rather than narrative examples.

**Exit signal:** a new contributor or maintainer can understand the current product, perform one bounded real contribution, and follow both technical and organizational provenance through accepted public surfaces.

## Phase 3 — Repeatable Product And Contributor Loop — Then

**Outcome:** Tiinex is not merely understandable once; its product and open-source operation can repeat useful cycles without relying on one person's hidden context.

**Existing anchors that may carry work into this phase:**

- [Artifact Lineage And Provenance Comprehension](001-3-2-artifact-lineage-provenance-comprehension-task.trace.md)
- [Public Trust Contribution And Governance](001-7-public-trust-contribution-governance-task.trace.md)
- [Funding Readiness And Financial Provenance](../financing/001-2-funding-readiness-and-financial-provenance.trace.md)

**Not yet materialized as dedicated work packages:** repeated contributor-cycle qualification, real-event invoice/payment/balance/tax-estimate exercises, and evidence-led adoption/usability follow-up. They should become Epics/Tasks only when the underlying work is concrete enough to deserve its own authority artifact.

**Exit signal:** multiple real cycles can be inspected end-to-end and the organization can identify what worked, what failed, what changed, and why without reconstructing the story from chats.

## Phase 4 — Sustainable Project Operation And Evidence-Led Adoption — Later

**Outcome:** Tiinex can sustain focused development and public stewardship without assuming indefinite uncompensated founder labor.

**Existing resource/funding anchors:**

- [Sustainable Dedicated Founder Capacity](../financing/001-2-1-sustainable-founder-capacity-resource-need.trace.md)
- [Funding Readiness And Financial Provenance](../financing/001-2-funding-readiness-and-financial-provenance.trace.md)

Later support, governance, external expertise, infrastructure, contributor support, or commercial/legal work should not be pre-created merely to fill this phase. They become work packages when evidence and real resource needs justify them.

**Exit signal:** Tiinex has a credible sustainable operating model supported by real funding/resource provenance and repeated external use or contribution evidence. This roadmap does not pre-commit which legal or commercial structure, if any, will be appropriate then.

## Roadmap Change Boundary

A work artifact changing from active to blocked, accepted, or done does **not** require a Roadmap edit. That state belongs to the work artifact and is surfaced through Roadmap Follow.

The Roadmap should change only when the organizational plan itself changes: a work package enters or leaves a phase, phase ordering changes, an exit intent changes, or evidence justifies replacing part of the sequence.

This separation is deliberate so routine Epic progress cannot make a duplicated Roadmap status table stale.

## Funding And Capacity Relationship

The founder has already invested several months of concentrated work and is willing to continue through an initial uncompensated period. That provides current momentum but is not treated as unlimited runway.

The earliest material funding milestone is therefore **sustainable dedicated founder capacity**: turning uncertain uncompensated availability into predictable focused project capacity. Later funding should be tied to concrete roadmap needs and outcomes rather than abstract growth targets.

See [Funding Readiness And Financial Provenance](../financing/001-2-funding-readiness-and-financial-provenance.trace.md) for the economic boundary.

## Interpretation Limits

This is an organizational sequence and relation surface, not a guaranteed delivery calendar, live status database, investor return forecast, release promise, staffing plan, or claim that every listed phase will remain unchanged. New evidence may reorder or replace later phases. Linked artifacts remain authoritative for their own status, blockers, acceptance, and implementation detail.
