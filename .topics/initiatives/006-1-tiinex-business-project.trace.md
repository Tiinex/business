# Tiinex Business

## Project Identity

- Description: Project for establishing the sustainable organizational, governance, financing, public-trust, contribution, and external operating outcomes needed for Tiinex to function as more than a technical artifact collection.
- Boundary: Business owns organizational and commercial outcomes. It is a sibling Project to Core, Tooling, Viewer, and Playthings, not the universal semantic Parent of all Tiinex work.

## Project Purpose And Scope

- Description: Make Tiinex organizational intent, responsibility, funding/resource boundaries, public contribution expectations, and durable operating decisions explicit enough to support real technical and product Projects without becoming a duplicate implementation tracker.
- Boundary: Technical implementation belongs to the Project/Task that owns that outcome in its natural Workspace; Business Project work does not absorb Core semantics, Tooling mechanics, Viewer UX, or product implementation merely because they matter organizationally.

## Parties And Resources

- Relevant Parties: Sigma and qualified Tiinex organizational Roles, contributors, partners, and future external parties where explicit Business work requires them.
- Relevant Resources: organization, financing, Roles, Decisions, public-trust/contribution governance, use-cases, Work Lifecycle, and qualified Project outcome material.

## Coordination State

- Description: Durable Business direction is established; concrete Business execution remains bounded and is spawned only when a real organizational outcome or decision frontier exists.
- Boundary: Historical Business Tasks are not reactivated by this Project. Task children should be used for ordinary decomposition; child Projects require independent coordinated outcomes.

## Milestones And Outcomes

- Description: Sustainable governance, contribution/public-trust mechanics, financing/resource clarity, role/accountability surfaces, and understandable Business disposition for cross-Project outcomes.
- Boundary: These are direction-level outcomes, not claims that any specific financing, governance, or public launch milestone is currently complete or active.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: legal status, funding, public readiness, contributor acceptance, commercial viability, or completion of historical Business work.
- Must Not Be Treated As: organizational identity itself, a mandatory intermediary between every Task and its real governing Project, or a generic catch-all for cross-Workspace work.
