## Accepted Mechanics

- Without an explicit consuming-session Role assertion, the selected Anchor recipient remains `grounded-to-discuss` even when the Anchor Role itself is qualified.
- A matching explicit `Anchor` session assertion is input/provenance only and does not self-authorize.

- With both matching assertion and qualified assignment-mode authorization present, bounded route readiness may become `grounded-to-act`.
