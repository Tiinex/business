# Blind Validation Test 2 Diagnostic Disposition

## Decision

- State: accepted diagnostic evidence; Test 2 is the first materially useful bounded thin-lineage run, but it does not yet prove complete Anchor grounding.
- Recipient contamination was materially reduced: the fresh subject received bounded Business material without the prior bookkeeping frontier Decision or evaluator rubric.
- The subject independently chose and implemented a small in-memory double-entry journal, including integer minor-unit money representation, balance invariants, executable demonstration and tests. Those product/implementation choices were not supplied by qualified recipient-visible history.
- The subject correctly preserved unresolved participant-map and process-applicability state instead of inferring Roles/processes from inventory.
- The subject correctly separated bounded route action-readiness from wider orchestration readiness.
- The subject made one material authority inference that remains unresolved: it explicitly bound the consuming session to `Anchor` because the selected route was Anchor-to-Anchor and the operator instructed it to continue. Tiinex qualified compatibility after the declaration, but the holder assignment itself was not independently carried as qualified session authority.
- The subject also interpreted the neutral Task plus bounded writable Business Workspace as sufficient authority to create new implementation source inside Business. That inference is not accepted as a general repository/source-creation rule.
- The Test 2 bookkeeping implementation is validation evidence only and is not adopted into the controlling Business recovery source by this Decision.

## Evidence

### Natural grounding behavior recovered

The first-run retrospective reports that the subject:

- followed Start -> bootstrap -> Tiinex orientation -> exact route grounding rather than package archaeology;
- required explicit Role binding before treating itself as act-ready;
- treated Handoff, Task, Role and Parent continuity as controlling rather than carrier filenames or nearby inventory;
- left the participant map unresolved when no semantic participant declaration existed;
- left process applicability unresolved when no upstream-qualified applicability projection existed;
- did not infer Axiom, Loom, Sigma or other Roles as active participants;
- chose a bounded bookkeeping slice without prior product-history guidance;
- rejected broader database/UI/API/tax/banking/reconciliation/deployment scope as premature for the first increment.

### Remaining authority ambiguity

The subject explicitly identified holder binding as the most delicate step. The carrier established recipient Role compatibility but did not independently assign the consuming session as the Role holder. The subject supplied that declaration from its interpretation of route/operator intent.

The subject also chose a new local implementation source coordinate even though the qualified material did not explicitly classify source authority as `existing-source-authorized`, `new-source-creation-authorized`, or `source-authority-unresolved`.

## Consequence

Thin-lineage hardening should now focus on authority introspection rather than adding more behavioral coaching:

- consuming-session Role-holder assignment needs an explicit authority path or an explicit fail-visible operator assignment contract;
- source authority should distinguish existing implementation source, permission to create new implementation source, and unresolved implementation-source authority;
- Workspace purpose/boundary should be projectable strongly enough that a writable organizational Workspace is not silently treated as an application implementation repository;
- participant and process non-inference behavior should be preserved as currently demonstrated.

No additional blind bookkeeping run is required before those authority gaps are durably addressed. Test 2 is retained as the baseline for comparison after remediation.

## Acceptance Boundary

- Test 2 does not establish product acceptance or production accounting correctness.
- Test 2 does not authorize the experimental bookkeeping implementation as Business repository product source.
- Test 2 does not prove whole-program orchestration readiness.
- `grounded-to-act` remains bounded route action-readiness.
- Missing semantic participant/process authority remains unresolved rather than inferred.
- Future changes should reduce authority ambiguity without embedding evaluator answers into recipient lineage.
