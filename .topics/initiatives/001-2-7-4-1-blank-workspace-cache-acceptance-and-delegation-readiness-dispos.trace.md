# Blank-Workspace Cache Acceptance And Delegation Readiness Disposition

## Decision

- State: accepted-with-next-hardening-front
- Subject: blank/minimal Workspace + route-bounded cache/Role grounding and the remaining delegation-readiness gap
- Decision: accept the Axiom semantic disposition and Loom Core mechanics for blank/minimal Workspace grounding as the current qualified basis. Open one bounded follow-up front for qualified delegation orchestration because the Site/Playthings secondary Anchor identified a relevant specialist Role but attempted to delegate by plain fresh-chat prompt rather than through a qualified Tiinex Task/Handoff/carrier/return chain.

## Accepted Grounding Contract

- Cache/representation establishes exact material identity, provenance, availability and coverage only.
- Exact cached semantic artifacts retain only their native semantics.
- Participant relevance, process applicability/requiredness, holder assignment and implementation-source authority require explicit qualified current-work authority plus a forward selector.
- Recipient Role material may ground recipient compatibility and bounded holder-assignment semantics without proving semantic participation or durable holder identity.
- Blank/minimal Workspace grounding may be act-ready when the smallest qualified forward-selected closure is sufficient; omitted sibling/repository material stays unknown/unavailable rather than reconstructed.
- Ordinary return allocation remains machine-derived from qualified pointer topology.

## Observed Remaining Gap

The first Site/Playthings secondary-Anchor production run demonstrated correct local authority discipline, but when a Site-owned defect required specialist investigation the Anchor proposed creating a fresh Kodax chat and sending a prose prompt. Sigma had to explain that specialist work should be expressed as a repo-local qualified Task and a Tiinex Handoff/carrier with lineage, authority and return path.

This means participant/capability recognition alone is insufficient. A production Anchor must also understand the canonical delegation chain and must not substitute ad-hoc chat prompting for Tiinex transport.

## Delegation Readiness Acceptance

A bounded Anchor is delegation-ready only when explicit qualified current-work authority establishes a relevant delegate/capability and the Anchor can:

1. preserve Business-root/Epic provenance while placing specialist work in the owning repository;
2. author a bounded specialist Task with exact scope, dependencies and authority limits;
3. author/manufacture a qualified Handoff/carrier to the selected Role rather than sending an unqualified prose prompt;
4. preserve sibling Workspace and Business mutation boundaries;
5. receive a qualified return and reconcile it against the common basis before disposition;
6. leave unresolved any missing delegate, process, source or transport authority instead of inventing it.

## Boundary

This Decision does not make every available Role a participant, does not require delegation when Anchor-owned work is rational, does not grant source mutation authority, and does not itself define a new canonical delegation schema. Prefer existing Task/Handoff/Relation/process semantics unless specialists prove a missing primitive.
