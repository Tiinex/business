# Business Repository-Only Cold-Start Acceptance Decision

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: Foundation Business repository-only semantic cold-start gate
- Decision: accept and close the Business repository-only semantic cold-start gate. The clean Sigma-run isolated test materially satisfied the Business Done criteria: a fresh model could orient from Business alone with useful current-versus-unknown boundaries, and Sigma explicitly returned PASS.

## Basis

- The tested model received the Business repository as the semantic input rather than Docs, Site, prior Tiinex conversation, or the operator Handoff package.
- Anchor's evidence review found the returned model materially recovered Tiinex identity, Foundation state, roles/authority, current priorities, and important uncertainty boundaries without upgrading unsupported product, legal, staffing, financing, or completion claims into fact.
- Sigma then performed the retained human acceptance duty and returned PASS.
- Sigma explicitly noted that bootstrap was not accessible in this run, so the test could not judge Tooling bootstrap friendliness, context cost, or comparative efficiency.

## Consequences

- Business repository-only semantic self-grounding is no longer an open Foundation acceptance blocker.
- Bootstrap accessibility, common CLI ergonomics, and LLM context/token efficiency remain a separate Tooling problem and may not be claimed from this acceptance.
- Viewer PoC parity, public trust/projection closure, strict host-dependent closure, and final Foundation acceptance remain separate gates.
- The accepted Business cold-start lineage may be reduced only under the normal landed/accepted/reduction process; this Decision alone does not authorize remote cleanup.

## Review Conditions

- Reopen this gate only if a later materially changed Business first-contact surface regresses cold-reader truthfulness, or a genuinely isolated repeat test exposes a new semantic self-grounding failure. Do not reopen it merely because Tooling/bootstrap ergonomics remain unfinished.
