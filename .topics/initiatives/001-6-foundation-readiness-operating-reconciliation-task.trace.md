# Foundation Readiness And Operating Reconciliation

## Objective

Make Tiinex organizationally understandable, publicly honest, recoverable, and human-usable enough that ordinary development can resume from a stable foundation rather than from hidden context or historical accumulation.

## Done Criteria

- Business shows a small current frontier with owner/Role, dependency, expected outcome, and Done gate at a level a non-developer manager can understand.
- Business leaf-first discovery remains trustworthy: stable leaves are current organizational truth, and `business-development/` exposes at most one active lineage per currently working Role rather than accumulated completed work.
- Business itself passes repository-only semantic cold start: an isolated general LLM given only the current `Tiinex/business` repository can recover Tiinex identity, current state, priorities, boundaries, resources, and unknowns without Handoff, Handoff package, Anchor pre-context, or Tooling bootstrap.
- A CEO can understand and direct the current organizational picture through ordinary repository/web/LLM surfaces, including on mobile; local IDE/terminal/remote-PC access is an escalation path for technical execution or deep inspection rather than a prerequisite for executive comprehension.
- Detailed implementation work lives in Docs, Site, or Tooling near the code or semantics it changes; a human developer can understand that local Task without LLM support and follow Parent upward for wider context.
- Current organizational Topic/Task lineage is truthful, dimension-path aligned with declared Parent ancestry, and reachable to `.topics/001-tiinex.trace.md` where organizational lineage applies.
- The public challenge/bounty is closed fairly under its published boundary, with result and payout evidence preserved.
- Public `.github`, READMEs, repository metadata, and website copy are reconciled from current provenance rather than used as competing authority.
- Human-product quality is judged against observed PoC value as well as semantic correctness: important interaction patterns are preserved, intentionally improved, or explicitly rejected with reason.
- Tooling iteration friction is measured before optimization; focused meaningful checks are preferred during bounded work and broader gates have an explicit purpose.
- A stable successor carrier is accepted only with complete qualified Business, Docs, and Site Workspaces together and successful cold-start recovery.
- Sigma can inspect the resulting foundation and accept that it is understandable, truthful, recoverable, and professionally presentable before ordinary feature expansion is thawed.

## Scope

- Organization/current-truth cleanup, manager-readable portfolio state, external obligations, public/release readiness, human-product grounding, and the minimum blocker work needed for a stable foundation.
- Business records organizational outcomes and gates, not code-level subtasks.
- Historical material remains recoverable through Git; current HEAD may remove executed or superseded intermediate artifacts when they no longer govern future behavior.
- Do not introduce a new dashboard/status schema solely to summarize the portfolio.
- Do not optimize wording or workflow to bypass host controls; treat observed host wait as an external measurement dimension until evidence supports a stronger conclusion.

## Dependencies

- Portfolio sequence: [Tiinex Roadmap](001-5-roadmap.trace.md).
- Organization root: [Tiinex](../001-tiinex.trace.md).
- Current Business, Docs, and Site snapshots and qualified Tooling used for discovery/validation.
- Sigma for human priority, bounty/payout decisions, product observation, and final foundation acceptance.
- Loom for bounded implementation when a local technical Task is clear; Axiom only for demonstrated semantic gaps.

## Current Operating Picture

### Active

- **Foundation Readiness** — Anchor coordinates current truth and dependency ordering; Sigma supplies human acceptance.
- **Business repository-only cold start** — make Business semantically self-sufficient for a blank LLM and final isolated quality testing.
- **Public challenge closure** — externally time-bound and must be closed fairly before it disappears from current work.
- **Tooling iteration efficiency** — measure and simplify the development loop without broad feature expansion.
- **Human navigation baseline** — preserve the strongest observed PoC comprehension patterns and make Parent/lineage orientation usable without LLM support.

### Next

- **Public trust and repository surfaces** — reconcile `.github`, READMEs, contribution/security/support material, repository metadata, and website copy after Business truth is settled.
- **Stable cold-start contract** — qualify the Business + Docs + Site three-workspace rule before creating the next stable carrier major.
- **Artifact/lineage comprehension** — strengthen how humans understand provenance once the navigation baseline is grounded.

### Hold

- Broad Core, Tooling, Viewer, or unrelated feature expansion that is not required to close a demonstrated foundation blocker.
- New schemas created only to organize work.
- Test proliferation or broad deletion without a distinct contract/risk and replacement evidence.

## Known Foundation Risks

- Public Site current/release policy and its qualifying pipeline need explicit reconciliation before stronger release claims.
- Current public presentation surfaces may lag `.topics` provenance and should be treated as projections until reconciled.
- Tooling iteration is affected by both Tiinex validation/test cost and externally visible host wait; current observations do not prove one hidden cause.
- Branch protection and required-check posture on key public repositories remains a later governance/public-trust item rather than an assumed guarantee.
