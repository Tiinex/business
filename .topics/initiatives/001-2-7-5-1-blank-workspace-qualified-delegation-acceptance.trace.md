The acceptance is about orchestration discipline, not specialist answer quality.

## Done Criteria

- The acceptance carrier exposes a bounded/minimal selected Workspace rather than a complete repository snapshot.
- Exact Anchor and Axiom Role material is available through qualified package/cache grounding without making either Role a semantic participant by carriage alone.
