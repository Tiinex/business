# Site + Playthings Secondary Anchor Production Trial

## Objective

Run one bounded production-grade secondary Anchor lane over the current Site + Verse Playthings integration frontier, using the now-accepted thin-lineage grounding mechanics to advance the real-browser/Sigma-test readiness work without consuming Master Anchor turns for every specialist step.

## Current Product Frontier

- Verse Playthings Major 003 remains a readiness/qualification major whose target is a genuinely viewable, technically qualified candidate through the real Site/App browser host before Sigma human acceptance.
- Site Major 005 has already narrowed the remaining technical path to the self-contained local-source browser harness and its bounded Sigma real-browser execution gate.
- Site and Playthings remain separate repository ownership domains even when one secondary Anchor coordinates them.

## Operating Outcome

- A fresh secondary Anchor recovers the current Site and Playthings state without Master-chat reconstruction.
- The secondary Anchor creates/uses repository-local Tasks for Site and Playthings that trace to this Business Epic and preserves their existing repository-local lineage.
- The secondary Anchor may delegate bounded specialist work inside the lane, reconcile returns, and prepare exact human-test material where required.
- The lane returns qualified state, blockers, and recovery/checkpoint material to Master Anchor for Business integration and cross-lane disposition.
- Master Anchor remains the only writer of Business.

## Boundaries

- No silent widening into unrelated Core/App/Docs/VS Code work; sibling-repository changes become explicit owner-routed blockers/delegations unless separately authorized.
- Technical browser PASS is not Sigma product/UX acceptance.
- Existing human gates remain human gates; the secondary Anchor may make them deterministic and low-friction but may not fabricate PASS/acceptance.
- Carrier lineage is local to each prefix/discriminator and machine-derived on the ordinary non-Major path; artifact lineage and project/chat instance numbers remain separate.
- This Epic authorizes one bounded production trial of secondary-Anchor orchestration, not permanent independent Business authority.
