# Public Provenance And Reference Experience

## Objective

Provide a credible public reference experience showing what Tiinex is, how provenance remains readable, what is current, and how real artifacts can be inspected without exposing internal scaffolding as the product story.

## Done Criteria

- A first-contact visitor can understand Tiinex identity and inspect representative real artifacts/lineages.
- Public examples demonstrate provenance, continuity, boundaries, and recoverability rather than only feature claims.
- Current project status and public trust surfaces are reachable without presenting historical development noise as active product structure.
- The public Viewer remains a reference implementation, not the sole definition of Tiinex.

## Scope

- Do not optimize examples so heavily that they stop representing real Tiinex behavior.
- Do not publish private or unsafe material merely for completeness.
- Do not turn marketing copy into semantic authority.

## Dependencies

- Business public-trust epic.
- Human navigation and artifact comprehension epics.
- Qualified public Docs and Site surfaces.
