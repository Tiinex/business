# Blind Validation Test 0 / Test 1 Diagnostic Disposition

## Decision

- State: accepted diagnostic evidence; neither Test 0 nor Test 1 proves unassisted thin-lineage orchestration grounding.
- Test 0: explicit-policy contaminated. The recipient Role/Handoff/grounding context directly carried most of the behaviors being evaluated, so the run is retained as a positive control for policy-following rather than a blind grounding pass.
- Test 1: historical-context contaminated. The current Handoff and Task were materially more neutral, but complete Business carriage exposed earlier bookkeeping frontier history. The fresh Anchor explicitly reported using that historical Decision as the strongest product prior.
- Test 1 additionally exposed an unresolved source-authority boundary: the fresh Anchor inferred that a new current Task plus a complete carried Business workspace authorized creation of new implementation source inside Business. That inference is not accepted as a general source-authority rule.
- Test 1 implementation is validation evidence only. The bookkeeping-pilot source created inside the test return is not adopted into the controlling Business recovery state by this Decision.

## Evidence

### Test 0

The fresh subject correctly followed bounded route authority, participant/process non-inference and exact-source blocker discipline, but its retrospective stated that those behaviors were unusually explicit and strongly scaffolded by the received Role/Handoff/Decision context. Its independently chosen product content was the journal-entry frontier itself.

### Test 1

The fresh subject independently produced and tested a small journal-entry validator, but its retrospective stated that it found and reused the prior `Bookkeeping Pilot — First Delivery Frontier Disposition` from complete Business history. It also identified its largest inferential step as choosing `bookkeeping-pilot/` as a new implementation source location inside the carried Business workspace.

## Consequence

The next blind variant must remove both contamination channels:

- evaluator/grounding policy must not be present in recipient controlling or reference context; and
- unrelated historical Business artifacts must not be available as ordinary Workspace inventory.

A bounded Business representation is therefore the preferred next test shape. It may carry only the neutral product Epic/Task, baseline Anchor Role, selected Handoff, exact Workspace identity material and the detached Parent closure mechanically required for qualification. Detached Parent recovery must not be promoted into recipient work guidance.

## Source-Authority Observation

Grounding needs a machine-readable distinction between at least:

- existing implementation source explicitly authorized;
- new implementation-source creation explicitly authorized; and
- implementation source authority unresolved.

A complete Workspace archive by itself must not silently collapse those states.

## Acceptance Boundary

- These findings improve test design; they do not change `grounded-to-act` semantics.
- No new participant or process schema is authorized here.
- No test implementation inside Business is accepted as Business product source.
- A future pass must be graded from recipient-visible qualified material plus retrospective, not from whether it happens to produce useful code.
