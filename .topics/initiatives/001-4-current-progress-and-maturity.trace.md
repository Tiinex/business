# Tiinex Current Progress And Maturity

## Executive Read

Tiinex is **past the idea-only stage**. It already has a substantial artifact/provenance model, maintained public schema material, working Tooling and recovery mechanics, public repositories, a human-tested Viewer proof of concept, and a current Business operating structure.

Tiinex is **not yet at a fully qualified stable public product baseline**. The present Foundation Readiness phase exists to reconcile the parts that already work, make public and organizational truth coherent, preserve proven human value through refactoring, qualify recovery/cold-start behavior, and remove ambiguity before broad feature development resumes.

No percentage-complete claim is made. Different Tiinex surfaces have different maturity and qualification states.

## Progress Already Demonstrated

- **Artifact and provenance semantics:** Tiinex has a maintained public schema corpus covering core provenance, coordination, discovery, resources, financial instruments, validation, workspaces, lineage-related behavior, and other bounded artifact roles. Schema existence does not imply every runtime companion or product flow is complete.
- **Readable lineage model:** current Business artifacts use explicit Parent continuity and integrity so detailed work can be followed toward broader project context without depending on folder proximity or chat history.
- **Tooling and recoverability:** portable Tooling exists for discovery, validation, integrity, lineage resolution, package/recovery operations, and cold-start qualification. Exact runtime support is not uniform across every readable schema family.
- **Human product evidence:** an earlier Viewer PoC demonstrated useful workspace, tree, lineage, history, creation, import/export, and degraded-state comprehension patterns. Those observations are product evidence, not current runtime authority.
- **Current Viewer/refactor work:** a newer Site refactor exists and is the current implementation direction, but release/current-branch policy and a known validation pipeline failure remain Foundation risks.
- **Organizational foundation:** Business now carries an organization root, Roles, Initiatives, financing boundaries, public obligations, a current Foundation gate, executive grounding, and repository-only LLM acceptance criteria.
- **Mobile/PoC Business review:** a human review of the current Business source in the Tiinex PoC on a non-optimized mobile client found the overview usable enough to navigate and understand the organization. The review also exposed the lack of a clear progress view, roadmap, and finance/funding story; this artifact and its sibling roadmap are responses to that finding, not proof of broad mobile usability.
- **Founder execution evidence:** the founder reports several months of concentrated Tiinex work and accepts an initial uncompensated period to continue the project. Business does not currently contain an audited time ledger or monetary valuation of that historical work.

## Maturity By Area

### Core / Semantics — working foundation, still evolving

The artifact/provenance model is substantial enough to support real Business, Discovery, resource, financial, validation, workspace, and lineage artifacts. Remaining work includes qualification consistency, companion/runtime coverage, public explanation, and continued evidence-driven refinement rather than proving the semantic model is finished.

### Tooling — operationally useful, qualification uneven by capability

Portable Tooling has already supported real cold-start, integrity, lineage, packaging, and audit workflows. Some readable schema families still lack exact runtime companions or validators, and observed host wait must remain separate from local Tooling cost when efficiency is judged.

### Viewer / Site — product value demonstrated, current baseline not yet fully qualified

The PoC established recognizable human value. The current refactor is intended to preserve or improve those outcomes with cleaner reusable semantics/tooling, but a refactor is not accepted merely because its architecture is newer. Current pipeline/release ambiguity must be resolved before a blanket stable-release claim.

### Business / Organization — coherent foundation candidate

Business is now usable as a manager-readable organizational surface and has passed an initial mobile PoC review. Final human review, repository-only blank-LLM tests, public projection reconciliation, provider-neutral funding-channel discovery, and Foundation closure are still required before treating the organizational foundation as complete.

### Public open-source operation — partially ready

The project is public and licensed, but first-contact consistency, contribution/security/support/governance/funding presentation, stale-repository routing, and clear Site publication/current-state policy still need reconciliation.

### Financial sustainability — not established

The project has founder-contributed time and a founder pledge to fund the recorded 1000 SEK public bounty from personal funds, but Business does not establish receipt of that pledge, an authoritative project balance, secured external funding, budget/runway, sustainable founder compensation, or an active PayPal/Kickstarter/other provider channel. The project can continue initially through uncompensated founder capacity, but indefinite unpaid full-time work is not assumed as the sustainable operating model.

## What Foundation Is De-Risking Now

Foundation is not a restart of Tiinex. It is a consolidation gate over work that already exists.

The current de-risking work is to make sure that:

- Business can explain Tiinex truthfully to a new human or blank LLM without hidden pre-context;
- public repository and website first-contact claims agree with current provenance;
- the public challenge is closed fairly and its financial commitment is traceable;
- important PoC human outcomes survive or are explicitly changed with reason;
- Tooling/recovery behavior is qualified without pretending host latency has a known hidden cause;
- Business, Docs, and Site can be carried together through a stable cold-start boundary;
- broad feature development resumes from a coherent baseline instead of adding more drift.

## How To Read “How Far Along?”

A useful short answer is:

> Tiinex already has real semantics, tooling, public code, a demonstrated Viewer PoC, and a functioning organizational provenance model. It is currently consolidating those pieces into a qualified public foundation before broad feature development resumes. Financial sustainability and broader adoption are later maturity gates, not current claims.

## Interpretation Limits

This maturity view is an evidence-bounded organizational synthesis. It does not prove production readiness, market fit, adoption scale, commercial viability, audited founder hours, release qualification, accounting correctness, or a numerical completion percentage. More specific current artifacts own their own status and should correct this synthesis if it drifts.
