The target is the exact carrier shape required by the acceptance test, not a synthetic-only fixture.

## Done Criteria

- A bounded/minimal Handoff package can declare one non-endpoint participant Role pointer for grounding/cache carriage and manufacture/roundtrip without `cache-over-expansion`.

- Missing or unqualified Role/cache material remains fail-closed with an exact blocker.

## Scope
