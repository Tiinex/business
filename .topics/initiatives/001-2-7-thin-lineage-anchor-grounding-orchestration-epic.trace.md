# Thin-Lineage Anchor Grounding And Orchestration Reliability Epic

## Current Read

A richly carried successor can recover well while still depending on lineage that a sparse real-world Handoff may not provide. The current objective is to make a fresh Anchor recover its own Role, the relevant participant/capability landscape, source-authority boundaries, a stable work frontier, delegation choices, human gates and recovery obligations from the smallest qualified material that legitimately carries those facts.

This Topic is Epic-shaped Business coordination authority. Detailed semantic work belongs in Docs, shared portable mechanics belong in Core/Tooling, host implementation belongs in the owning host repository, and blind successor validation is performed by an isolated Anchor session rather than by the already-grounded controlling Anchor.

## Outcome Contract

- A fresh Anchor distinguishes bounded `grounded-to-act` authority from being sufficiently grounded to orchestrate the wider work.
- Anchor resolves its own current Role and obtains or explicitly bounds the participant/capability map needed for the current task before substantive planning.
- Participant discovery preserves the distinction between an available Role artifact and an actually relevant/related participant; transport presence, chat presence and filenames do not create delegation or holder authority.
- Source grounding works across the qualified source forms Tiinex actually carries, including complete Workspace material and bounded/cache material, without assuming Business is physically present in every package.
- Missing authoritative material produces an exact blocker to the appropriate operator/participant rather than an opportunistic connector/repository expedition.
- Anchor treats scarce/slow host turns and volatile local working storage as operating constraints when evidenced by the active host/operator, uses bounded parallelism through Roles, avoids silent scope growth and establishes full recovery at stable progression boundaries.
- Detailed repository-local Tasks and their returns retain a traceable Parent path through this Epic to `Tiinex Tooling -> Initiatives -> Tiinex`.
- A truly fresh Anchor passes a blind thin-lineage validation without Sigma reconstructing the organization, work plan or role system for it.

## Workstreams

- **Semantic/Role grounding:** Axiom/Docs reviews the participant, source-authority and orchestration-readiness model and returns the smallest durable semantic/Role/process delta.
- **Portable Tooling projection:** Loom/Core audits and hardens machine-readable projection of already-qualified role/participant/source facts and exact blockers without inventing new semantics.
- **Host/product follow-on:** host-specific work is opened only if the shared returns prove a host-local gap; it receives its own repository-local Task under this Epic.
- **Blind validation:** after the accepted deltas land, a fresh Anchor receives a deliberately thin carrier and is graded on independent organization recovery, routing, delegation and recovery behavior.

## Boundaries

- Business is the organizational coordination root, not the storage location for specialist implementation details.
- Only Anchor mutates Business for this initiative. Specialist Roles return recommendations/evidence; they do not write Business authority.
- Existing grounding/recovery/process lineages remain evidence and context. They are not mass-reparented merely to create a cleaner diagram.
- No new schema family is authorized by this Epic. Axiom must establish a semantic need before schema work opens.
- Carrier Major numbering, artifact lineage dimensions and Sigma's local ChatGPT project instance numbers are separate namespaces and must not be inferred from one another.

## Completion Gate

This Epic is not complete when prose exists or one richly-grounded Anchor behaves well. Completion requires one or more genuinely fresh thin-lineage successors to recover the intended operating model with negligible Sigma correction, use available specialist Roles appropriately, preserve authority boundaries, and leave a recovery state from which another fresh Anchor can continue.
