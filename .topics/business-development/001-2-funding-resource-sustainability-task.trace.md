# Funding, Resource And Sustainability Model

## Objective

Create a transparent, lightweight model for support, contributions, costs, resource needs, and project sustainability that works before and after any future legal business entity exists.

## Done Criteria

- Funding/support artifacts preserve factual amounts, dates, sources, fees, currencies, purpose, and relevant initiative links when real events occur.
- The model distinguishes project support facts from tax, legal, accounting, investment, or governance conclusions it cannot prove.
- Resource needs can be related to Initiatives and meaningful deliverables without granting sponsors automatic roadmap authority.
- Sigma can review sustainability from durable evidence rather than reconstructing platform histories.

## Scope

- Do not require incorporation or simulate accounting software.
- Do not classify taxes or legal status inside provenance artifacts unless separately supported.
- Apache-2.0 project licensing remains independent from sponsorship/support relationships.

## Dependencies

- Existing financing lineage may be preserved as historical input and reconciled rather than silently replaced.
- Real funding/resource events are required before detailed process automation is justified.
