# Sigma Audience Reach And External Learning Feedback

## Observed Signal

- Sigma reported that Tiinex had accumulated substantial dogfooding and technical progress without a correspondingly useful external audience or feedback loop. The concern was not a popularity target; it was the lack of informed outside dialogue capable of challenging assumptions, comparing Tiinex with prior art, and exposing unnecessary reinvention or missed failure cases.

## Source

- Source: direct Sigma observation during the current Tiinex collaboration.
- Follow-on context: Glimmer responded in its audience-facing capacity by suggesting targeted outreach to a relevant provenance/standards community rather than broad promotional outreach.

## Interpretation

- Interpretation: the project benefits from an expert external audience when that audience can provide comparison, critique, standards context, and independent failure cases. Glimmer's suggestion is advisory and human-facing; it does not create technical or semantic authority for Tiinex.

## Feedback Target

- Target: Tiinex public learning, outreach, audience-building, and external provenance/standards comparison practice.

## Feedback Received

- Preserve the original problem truthfully: Sigma wanted a real audience and external feedback surface because internal dogfooding alone cannot reveal every overlap, reinvention, or blind spot.
- Prefer targeted communities with genuine subject-matter overlap over generic promotion.
- Enter such communities in a learning posture: explain Tiinex clearly, ask where it overlaps existing work, invite correction, and avoid presenting participation as endorsement.
- Keep external feedback as provenance/input. Any future project-changing claim must still be qualified by the appropriate Tiinex semantic, process, or implementation owner.
- Treat the outreach itself as dogfood: human motivation, Glimmer suggestion, Sigma action, external response, and any later Tiinex change should remain distinguishable in lineage.

## Disposition

- State: accepted
- Follow-Up: Sigma performed a targeted introduction to a W3C provenance-focused community mailing list. Preserve that outreach as evidence beneath this feedback lineage and treat any response as new external input rather than retroactive validation.

## Limits

- Fidelity: professional durable representation of Sigma's direct motivation and the subsequent Glimmer suggestion; conversational phrasing about "not having an audience" is represented as an external-feedback and informed-audience gap rather than as a popularity claim.
- Boundary: this feedback does not claim that W3C, any community group, or any external participant has reviewed, accepted, endorsed, standardized, or validated Tiinex.
