# Tiinex Business Development

## Project Identity

- Description: Tiinex Business Development is the bounded coordinated effort to professionalize Tiinex project governance, portfolio structure, funding visibility, public trust, operating overview, and recoverable continuity.
- Boundary: This project develops how Tiinex is operated as an open-source project. It does not assert legal incorporation, employment, tax classification, investment status, or authority not separately established.

## Project Purpose And Scope

- Description: Create a small but institutionally credible operating structure that Sigma and AI roles can use repeatedly: stable Roles, real Initiatives, meaningful work packages, explicit funding/resource provenance, public trust surfaces, and later evidence-derived Processes.
- Boundary: Prefer additive, evidence-backed structure over corporate simulation. Historical artifact corpora remain preserved; current work should be condensed into explicit frontiers instead of rewritten for appearance.

## Parties And Resources

- Relevant Parties: Sigma for human structural judgment and priority; Anchor for architecture, continuity, review, and cross-role coherence; Axiom for schema/semantic gaps; Loom for shared Tooling implementation and qualification.
- Relevant Resources: The tiinex/business, tiinex/docs, and tiinex/site workspaces; Tiinex Tooling; public GitHub organization material; future support/funding records when real events exist.

## Coordination State

- Description: Active through Foundation Readiness. The immediate goal is not more Business taxonomy but a small current operating picture, fair closure of the public challenge, clear public-trust/repository projections, and a recoverable handoff boundary before ordinary feature expansion resumes.
- Boundary: Implementation details stay in Docs/Site/Tooling. Business records priority, dependency, owner, gate, external obligation, risk, and expected outcome at a level a non-developer manager can understand.

## Milestones And Outcomes

- Description: Near-term success is an inspectable Business authority surface, explicit foundation blockers, closed public obligations, human-readable cross-repository work, and a qualified recovery point that carries Business, Docs, and Site together.
- Boundary: A later Roadmap or Process artifact is created only when stable sequencing or repeated behavior justifies it; neither is needed merely to make the current portfolio look complete.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: legal incorporation, commercial viability, funding, tax treatment, governance authority, project completion, or acceptance by external contributors or sponsors
- Must Not Be Treated As: a company registration, investment prospectus, employment structure, accounting record, roadmap, or substitute for the product Initiatives
