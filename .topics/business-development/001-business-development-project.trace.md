# Tiinex Business Development

## Project Identity

- Description: Tiinex Business Development is the active workbench for changes that are currently improving the Business repository and its organizational/public operating model.
- Boundary: Durable organizational truth belongs in its natural stable branch such as Initiatives, Roles, Financing, Executive, or Decisions. This workbench must not become the place a normal external reader is expected to browse for the organization's permanent map.

## Project Purpose And Scope

- Description: Carry only bounded active improvements until their outcomes are accepted into the appropriate durable Business surface.
- Boundary: Keep at most **one active lineage per role currently working in Business Development**. A role may deepen its one lineage when needed, but must not create parallel sibling work streams merely to capture every thought. Once a role's work is complete, tested at its level, and its outcome has landed, that development lineage should leave current HEAD when no active descendant still needs it. Git history preserves the work provenance; current Business should not accumulate completed improvement exhaust.

## Parties And Resources

- Relevant Parties: Sigma for human structural judgment and priority; Anchor for architecture, continuity, review, and cross-role coherence; Axiom for schema/semantic gaps; Loom for shared Tooling implementation and qualification.
- Relevant Resources: The tiinex/business, tiinex/docs, and tiinex/site workspaces; Tiinex Tooling; public GitHub organization material; future support/funding records when real events exist.

## Coordination State

- Description: Active through Foundation Readiness as the bounded Business-repository workbench. This prepared landing has no active role child lineage; a later Business-specific change opens one bounded role lineage only when real work begins.
- Boundary: Implementation details and specialist provenance stay in their natural Docs/Site/Tooling domain. Durable Foundation, public-trust, Roadmap, progress/maturity, financing, initiative, role, executive, and accepted semantic outcomes live outside this workbench. A Business-development leaf must still be current, truthful, and understandable if it appears directly in a leaf-first Feed.

## Milestones And Outcomes

- Description: Near-term success is an inspectable Business authority surface, explicit foundation blockers, closed public obligations, human-readable cross-repository work, and a qualified recovery point that carries Business, Docs, and Site together.
- Boundary: Roadmap and durable organizational work packages belong with Initiatives because they outlive a repository-editing session. The current artifact-native Process representation experiment lives under `Processes` as explicitly proposed/local semantics; this workbench must not duplicate it or turn that experiment into a universal Process claim.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: legal incorporation, commercial viability, funding, tax treatment, governance authority, project completion, or acceptance by external contributors or sponsors
- Must Not Be Treated As: a company registration, investment prospectus, employment structure, accounting record, roadmap, or substitute for the product Initiatives
