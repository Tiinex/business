# Human And Machine Entry Consistency — Integrated Bounded Work

## Objective

Review the carried Business Workspace's human-facing repository onboarding against its qualified source authority, decide what if anything needs a narrowly authorized local correction, and preserve a verifiable continuation. Distinguish legitimately different audiences, actual contradictions, applicability of selected Process and Scaffold, and completed actions.

## Scope

- The Business Work Lifecycle Process is explicitly selected for this bounded local review. Recover its actual applicability, choose the relevant branch, and distinguish performed work from unexecuted lifecycle steps or human acceptance.
- Inspect the carried Business `README.md`, `llms.txt`, and relevant qualified Business organizational Decisions and Project/Task contracts. Evaluate the statements as human/machine entry surfaces, not as independent current-work or policy authority. Reach concrete conclusions from source material rather than assuming differences are defects.
- A narrowly justified edit to `README.md` is permitted **inside the qualified disposable Business continuation only** when an actual conflict with higher-authority qualified material is established. Preserve the intended human-facing explanatory role, scope, and unchanged unrelated wording. If no edit is justified, explain the no-op; do not edit other repository files merely to force activity.
- The Native Workspace Work Root Scaffold is explicitly selected **for read-only planning**, not for structural apply, against the carried Business root. Classify whether its required work-root structure is already satisfied; do not silently modify the structure.
- Author one local Tiinex Evidence artifact in this Task work area that states what was observed, which sources controlled, the actual outcome, exactly which canonical source bytes were edited if any, unchanged/no-op aspects, limitations and unresolved dependencies. Use the qualified Parent and normal Tooling authoring.
- Return the result to Sigma through the existing Handoff Package V1 route, without extra duplicates, scratch output, or premature Task completion or acceptance claims.

## Dependencies

- This Task and its incoming Handoff; the Business organizational Project/Task topology Decision; selected Business Work Lifecycle Process; selected Native Workspace Work Root Scaffold; the carried Business source files; Tiinex Tooling and exact recipient role authority.

## Done Criteria

- A justified disposition for the carried human/machine first-contact material, based on concrete qualified source evidence and correct ownership boundaries.
- Any performed local source edit is narrow, directly inspectable, and limited to the authorized Business continuation; any non-change is evidence-based.
- Read-only Work Root Scaffold applicability is evaluated separately from actual structural mutation.
- One causally parented qualified Evidence and one canonical return Handoff, with honest scope/authority limits, form the bounded result.

## Interpretation Limits

- The fixture is deliberately disposable experimental source, not authoritative published Tiinex source history. It is not permission to mutate GitHub, other Workspace carriers, schemas or historical signed ancestors.
- Exact byte-integrity checks qualify transport continuity, not independent semantic correctness or Sigma acceptance. A complete Process execution cannot be inferred from finding its definition.
