- Purpose: Perform the exact bounded Business onboarding-source review and a qualified local continuation under the selected Process and Scaffold; return one auditable result to Sigma.
- From: Sigma
- From Kind: role
- From Reference: [Sigma](business::.topics/roles/001-4-sigma-role.trace.md)
- To: Anchor
- To Kind: role
- To Reference: [Anchor](business::.topics/roles/001-1-anchor-role.trace.md)
- Notes: Experimental disposable Handoff under Sigmas direction; does not assert any permanent holder identity or human acceptance.

## Transfers

- integrated-first-contact-work
  - Transfer Kind: work-and-responsibility
  - Controlling Artifact: [Integrated bounded review Task](001-human-and-machine-entry-consistency-integrated-bounded-work.trace.md)
  - Description: Execute the controlling Task in its qualified local Business continuation. Evaluate human-facing Business README against the qualified Decisions, while distinguishing its intended audience from `llms.txt`. Apply a tightly bounded local correction only if supported by relevant authority, evaluate the selected Native Work Root Scaffold without treating it as structural apply authority, and record one causal Evidence before returning via Handoff V1.
  - Boundary: Only the qualified disposable Business continuation is writable. All other Workspaces are read-only. No published source mutation, semantic schema rewrite, status escalation or historical rearrangement.

  - Kind: excluded-scope
