## Objective

Qualify Tiinex grounding as a complete cold-start work system through reproducible experiments, implementation regression checks, semantic assessment, and handoff continuity. Keep tested evidence externally preserved, with compact Tiinex Evidence and Feedback artifacts recording exactly what each completed batch supports and what remains unresolved.

## Done Criteria

- Integrated scenarios demonstrate correct qualified work, justified changes and no-ops, source/role authority, Process and Scaffold applicability, causal lineage, and reproducible transfer across independent cold starts.
- Conclusions distinguish transport validity, semantic correctness, authorization, and human acceptance.
- Sigma explicitly determines the final grounding acceptance; package PASS does not imply Task closure.

## Scope

- Business owns cross-Workspace grounding validation and acceptance; actual implementation changes remain in their owning Workspaces and qualified task boundaries.
- One directory-local batch work area per substantively distinct experiment or remediation, with truthful Parent ancestry across directories.
- The original batch ZIPs, screenshots, and complete retros stay in external preservation; no raw test packages or expected/duplicate artifacts are imported.
- The earlier 036 clean-sweep remains historical; this Task does not retroactively complete, rename, or rewrite its signed lineage.
