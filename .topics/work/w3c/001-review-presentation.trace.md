# Review the published Tiinex presentation

**Status: proposed / blocked on approved publication and exact source pinning.**

## Objective

Offer an external technical reader a voluntary path to examine the general-purpose Tiinex presentation, ask questions, and return attributable observations or criticism without giving the reader permission to edit Tiinex's canonical project material.

## Concrete work

- Publish an editor-approved revision of the Presentation and Timeline artifacts in their owning repository.
- Record **exact commit-pinned artifact permalink(s)** for the reviewed revision; replace no citation silently when the target changes.
- Prepare a genuinely qualified, narrow recipient carrier containing the necessary presentation closure, this Task and an appropriate existing Role (candidate: Glimmer) **only after** checking current Role and Handoff contracts, endpoint selection and recipient scope.
- Offer the recipient ordinary text feedback as well as an optional artifact-based return. Record human words separately from any model summary or interpretation.
- Compare feedback to exact targets. Classify it as accepted, challenged, open or outside scope **only via the appropriate human decision process**, not by a model's confidence.

## Done Criteria

Completion is demonstrated by a fixed, reviewable presentation revision and returned feedback (or an explicit decline) under the stated boundaries. The following checks are required:



- A reader can understand the presentation without oral narration.
- The distributed source revision and its scope are unambiguous.
- Feedback includes exact target, observation and whether it is a human statement, AI interpretation or independently verified result.
- The reviewer can stop or decline without creating any Handoff or obligation.
- Any formal Handoff is actually qualified and lists bounded recipient responsibilities; none is implied by this draft Task.

## Scope

In scope: the presentation reader experience, source pinning, voluntary reviewer feedback, and its qualification for later consideration. Out of scope: changing canonical schemas, modifying host Tooling, granting membership/representation, accepting feedback as authoritative, or carrying out an unapproved Handoff.

## Dependencies

Blocked until maintainer approval, publication, qualification of recipient route and Role material. These prerequisites are not satisfied merely by authoring this Task.

## Open dependencies

- Maintainer approval of the V3 presentation.
- Immutable published revision.
- Checked recipient Role/assignment and necessary Workspace closure (Glimmer is **candidate**, not assigned here).
- Return-route details and consent/attribution choices for external feedback.

## Boundaries

This W3C path is one possible *use of* the presentation. The general Presentation and Timeline do **not** belong to W3C, imply group endorsement or originate from the meeting. The root of the Task may reference the Presentation Topic without asserting that feedback itself has the same Parent as the presentation. Feedback Target and a future Handoff each carry their own semantics. No remote publication or mutation is authorized by this draft.
