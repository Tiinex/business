# Checkpoint Package Discipline Feedback

## Observed Signal

- Sigma observed that Handoff Packages should be a normal working habit across Roles, not something produced only at large recovery points or when the platform is already near a limit.
- The desired operator contract is that natural checkpoints leave a durable package plus a compact statement of whether the conversation may branch and whether any human action is required.

## Source

- Source: Sigma
- Session Statement: all Roles should develop a habit of working through Tiinex lineages, clear handovers, and frequent Handoff/Recovery packages; packages need not always be full recovery points.

## Interpretation

- This is a continuity/process-design signal, not new Handoff authority.
- The likely reusable owner is the Tiinex Session Grounding And Continuity profile rather than duplicating identical checkpoint prose into every Role.
- Role material may still specialize return/handover behavior where a Role has distinct responsibility-transfer requirements.
- A checkpoint package without a qualified Handoff remains recovery/continuity material and must not be interpreted as responsibility transfer merely because a package exists.

## Feedback Target

- Target: `.topics/processes/session-grounding-and-continuity/001-session-grounding-and-continuity-process.trace.md`
- Scope: reusable checkpoint/handover discipline for Tiinex sessions and Role participants.

## Feedback Received

- Source: Sigma
- Feedback: make package production and explicit branch/action disposition a routine Tiinex working pattern across Roles so lineage/recovery practice becomes habitual and session limits are less dangerous.

## Disposition

- State: open-for-process-qualification
- Effect: preserve this requirement for the next Process review; do not silently retrofit every Role or claim the process is revised by this Feedback alone.
- Follow-Up: qualify the smallest reusable process rule covering natural checkpoint package manufacture and operator-facing `BRANCH?`, `HUMAN ACTION?`, and `NEXT?` disposition while preserving the distinction between recovery carriage and Handoff/work transfer.

## Limits

- This Feedback does not itself change Process applicability, Role authority, Handoff routing, recipient authority, acceptance, or remote-mutation authority.
- It does not require every small tool invocation to manufacture a package; the intended trigger is a natural durable checkpoint, handover/return, acceptance boundary, branch opportunity, or meaningful recoverability improvement.
