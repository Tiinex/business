# Tiinusen Tip Jar

## Instrument Identity

- Instrument Type: donation
- Instrument Role: voluntary personal appreciation support for Tiinusen
- Instrument Status: proposed

## Parties Or Roles

- Recipient Role: Tiinusen as the personal recipient
- Contributor Role: voluntary tipper; attribution may be public or private according to the actual payment/support surface

## Economic Terms

- Value Or Consideration: voluntary personal appreciation; no Tiinex ownership, governance right, feature priority, employment, sponsorship, or other project consideration is implied
- Use Boundary: personal support to the named recipient; not a Tiinex project fund unless a separate later contribution event explicitly moves resources into one

## Authority And Status

- Authority And Status: proposed support proposition; Business does not currently preserve a verified active payment endpoint, provider/channel binding, or evidence that an individual tip has been received
- Channel Boundary: no PayPal, Kickstarter, or other provider is implied by this artifact; provider-neutral channel behavior remains under [Funding Channels And Campaigns](../001-4-funding-channels-and-campaigns-discovery.trace.md) Discovery

## Resource Links

- Related Fund: none by default
- Possible Later Relation: a separately documented contribution may move personal resources toward General Fund, Bounty Fund, or another bounded project purpose; that event must not be inferred from this Tip Jar

## Interpretation Limits

- Interpretation Limits: this instrument does not prove receipt, payment-provider state, tax treatment, accounting correctness, legal rights, Tiinex project ownership, governance, feature priority, or project allocation.
