# Sustainable Dedicated Founder Capacity

## Need Statement

- Need: sustainable financial support for dedicated founder capacity so Tiinex can continue with focused, predictable development and organizational stewardship rather than depending indefinitely on uncompensated personal availability
- Need State: partially satisfied
- Priority: high after and during Foundation where continuity depends on concentrated founder attention
- Time Window: current and ongoing; no funded runway or deadline is established

## Required Resource

- Resource Kind: funds and organizational support
- Required Capability: make continued dedicated/full-time founder work financially sustainable while allowing Tiinex to remain an open-source project
- Quantity: unknown until an evidence-backed project budget, compensation boundary, tax/legal treatment, and funding form are established
- Preferred Form: predictable support tied to transparent project needs and roadmap outcomes rather than one-off assumptions about valuation or growth

## Required For

- Required For: continued Foundation closure, post-Foundation development thaw, public product qualification, contributor support, project continuity, and execution of the Tiinex roadmap at a focused pace
- Related Plan: [Tiinex Roadmap](../initiatives/001-5-roadmap.trace.md)

## Constraint Impact

- Constraint Impact: the founder is willing to continue through an initial uncompensated period, so work is not currently declared blocked. However, indefinite unpaid full-time contribution is not assumed. If sustainable support is not established, concentrated project capacity may eventually degrade toward intermittent spare-time work, increasing delivery time and continuity risk.

## Fulfillment Boundary

- Fulfillment Boundary: partial fulfillment is any real support that measurably reduces dependence on uncompensated founder capacity while preserving declared restrictions and provenance. Full fulfillment requires an evidence-backed funding/budget arrangement sufficient for predictable dedicated founder capacity; the amount and legal/tax form are intentionally not guessed here.
- Alternative Path: if funding remains unavailable, Tiinex may continue at lower or intermittent capacity; this is a slower continuity path, not evidence that the project or its existing work disappears.

## Interpretation Limits

- Limits: this need does not establish salary entitlement, employment, payroll, a personal living-cost claim, funding obligation, secured finance, runway, project valuation, tax treatment, or a specific amount. It describes an organizational capacity need and its consequence if unmet.
