# Voluntary Support And Tips

## Current Read

This branch carries low-friction appreciation/support routes that may be offered by a maintainer, contributor, initiative, or other clearly identified recipient. “Tip Jar” is acceptable public language; the provenance must still state who actually receives the money and whether the support is personal or project-directed.

## Support Route Rule

A Tip Jar is a support proposition/financial instrument, **not a fund, provider binding, or balance**. Each concrete jar must state its recipient/economic party, support purpose, consideration boundary, current status, relation (or non-relation) to Tiinex project funds, and what would count as receipt evidence.

Different jars may legitimately route to different destinations. A personal appreciation jar remains outside Tiinex project funds unless a separate contribution/receipt/allocation event moves money into a project fund. A project-directed support route may target General Fund or another bounded fund, but the route alone does not prove receipt. The provider/channel used to expose a jar should be discoverable separately rather than hardcoded into the jar's organizational meaning; see [Funding Channels And Campaigns](001-4-funding-channels-and-campaigns-discovery.trace.md).

## Participation Boundary

Additional people or initiatives may establish their own Tip Jar artifacts when there is a real support route to describe. Do not create jars merely to populate the tree, and do not infer that a tip grants ownership, governance, priority, employment, sponsorship, or other consideration unless separately established.

## Interpretation Limits

This branch does not prove that any payment endpoint is active, any tip has been received, any recipient has a particular tax treatment, or that personal support belongs to Tiinex.
