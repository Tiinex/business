# Tiinex Fresh-Start Executive Grounding

This topic is the compact current orientation after the project-wide cleanup and scaffold convergence pass.

## Current Read

Tiinex has no initiative execution declared active by this cleanup pass. Historical development attempts remain recoverable from immutable repository snapshots and qualified Reductions rather than being presented as current work.

The durable organizational root, roles, financing surfaces, reusable process authority, use cases, initiative root, fresh-start roadmap, and explicitly preserved unresolved obligations remain available for future work.

## Design Direction

A future execution thread starts with fresh grounding and an explicit current artifact. Historical lineage may be consulted as evidence or recovery material but must not be treated as current solely because a former leaf says ready/local, candidate/local, active/local, or similar lexical state.

## Next Artifacts

Create a new bounded Task, Project, Decision, or Topic only when there is concrete work to resume. Keep future reductions and repairs explicit rather than rebuilding a long implicit continuation chain.
