# A narrow, verifiable continuation is the better demonstration

A useful first experiment is modest. Give a recipient a bounded package and a clear entry. Ask them to inspect available Workspace(s), say what task is actually open, identify its source and authority, and either complete one permitted output or explain what blocks it. Then independently inspect the returned artifact, its evidence and Parent/target relationships.

## Suggested small exercise

1. Open a fresh session in a host that actually supports the required sandbox/tooling.
2. Attach the exact qualified carrier and its transport guidance.
3. Ask **what can be done with the material supplied**, without providing a hidden oral explanation.
4. Compare the response to a known expected frontier and explicitly inspect false assumptions.
5. If appropriate, make one small artifact and qualify it; preserve the returned ZIP and observation separately.

## Reviewer questions

- Was the declared intent recovered without smuggling it through the prompt?
- Were incomplete/unknown sources left visibly incomplete?
- Did the recipient treat the artifact's integrity as **integrity**, not truth?
- Was any action taken without explicit human authorization?
- Can a second reader reproduce the finding from the surviving artifacts?

## Current boundary

This describes a review pattern. It does **not** create a Handoff route for the reader and it does not grant a Role to an arbitrary external participant. A future reviewer-specific package may deliberately include a qualified Glimmer Role and bounded Task; that requires separate manufacture and review.
