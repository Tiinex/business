# Where does Tiinex go from here?

Tiinex did not start with a roadmap for a general-purpose system. It started with practical experiments, questions, and problems that kept leading to other problems.

Some experiments worked. Others reached their limits or failed to produce the outcomes I had hoped for. Each left something worth learning from.

Today, much of the work is focused on a particular question: **Can continuity be recovered reliably enough that work can move between environments without depending on the conversation in which it began?**

## The next experiments

If the ongoing grounding experiments establish a sufficiently dependable foundation, the next step is to begin expanding work across multiple initiatives in parallel. Some of those initiatives bring the story back to its beginnings: **the role-playing bot**, my personal website and social hub, and other artifact-driven projects that have been waiting while the underlying infrastructure evolved.

That parallelism will itself be a test. Continuing one bounded task is not the same as sustaining several concurrent initiatives without mixing their contexts, responsibilities and lineages.

Another important direction is improving the relationship between **schemas and their corresponding companions**. I want to move toward testable one-to-one parity between schema contracts and companion behavior where appropriate, supported by better tooling to generate, validate and maintain that relationship — potentially with substantial assistance from LLMs. This is an aspiration, not a claim that parity or schema-building tooling has already been delivered.

## Making the exploration sustainable

Another hope is to find a sustainable way to finance the experiment. Much of the work so far has depended on my own time and resources. Voluntary donations, sponsorship or other support might eventually make it possible to devote more consistent time to Tiinex without needing to divert that time elsewhere to sustain the work.

I also acquired several domains during the experiment, including **lineage.host, lineage.photos, lineage.press, lineage.training and lineage.works**. I have considered whether selling some of these names — individually, as a group or potentially at auction — could provide one-time funding for Tiinex. Another name I hold, **isyour.guru**, is a possible candidate for the same purpose. No sale method, valuation or commitment has been decided, and this should not be read as projected income. These possibilities come from my own account of the experiment, not an independent market assessment.

My intention is to keep Tiinex **freely accessible and open-source**. Funding should help sustain exploration, not create a new dependency on a paid platform or automatically give a sponsor authority over its direction. Whether such support will materialize remains uncertain.

## The direction, not a promised destination

None of these next steps is guaranteed. They are directions I intend to explore, not capabilities I claim to have delivered.

I do not want Tiinex to become another platform that people cannot leave. Components, tools and formats are not meant to be irreplaceable; better alternatives should be usable without losing the underlying principles.

**Perhaps the next important discovery will not be another feature of Tiinex, but what becomes possible when I no longer have to spend most of my time building Tiinex itself.**

I do not know exactly where that will lead.

So far, discovering the next problem has been a more reliable guide than pretending I already know the final destination.
