# Could a lineage be explored as a world?

The developer experimented with representing artifact lineage as a **navigable fantasy/world-like experience**. Instead of only listing documents in a feed or tree, an environment might let a reader move between places that express relationships.

The serious underlying question was whether a very different interface could keep the same meanings intact: what is a Parent, what is merely a link, what is current, what is unknown and where is the original source?

The fantasy-experiment chronology comes mainly from the developer's retrospective demonstration, not from a complete dated history of every build.

## Source-backed continuation

- [September Playthings lineage-reduction evidence](https://github.com/Tiinex/verse-playthings/blob/608237e2387be004e6bdee808618bc91866c3025/.topics/viewer/playthings/development/001-1-1-legacy-viewer-playthings-lineage-reduction-evidence.trace.md) preserves lessons from earlier Viewer-root experiments while warning against bringing all repetitive development history into the active source tree.

## Boundaries

A navigable world is a **projection**. It does not confer extra provenance on an artifact or establish that the visual display is a qualified representation of every schema relation. The September repository must not be asserted as the birth date of all fantasy experiments.
