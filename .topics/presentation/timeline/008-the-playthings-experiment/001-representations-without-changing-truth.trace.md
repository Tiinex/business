# Can an artifact lineage also become a navigable world?

Alongside the central Viewer and tooling work, the developer experimented with presenting lineage through a fantasy/world-like experience. The intended question was not whether fantasy graphics are a standard: **can radically different representations preserve the same underlying artifact relationships?**

The later Playthings repository gives a more durable separation of this exploratory UI/runtime work from the ordinary document viewer.

## Direct source artifact

- [September 8 legacy Viewer/Playthings lineage-reduction evidence](https://github.com/Tiinex/verse-playthings/blob/608237e2387be004e6bdee808618bc91866c3025/.topics/viewer/playthings/development/001-1-1-legacy-viewer-playthings-lineage-reduction-evidence.trace.md) — records the work of preserving and separating relevant Viewer/Playthings history during repository consolidation.

## Date and causality boundary

Some fantasy-Viewer experiments were described retrospectively during the October demonstration. The specific origins and sequence of every visual experiment are not established by this later September source alone. This chapter uses **September's repository separation as a documented anchor**, not as proof that the first experiment began then.

This is also not a claim that the role-playing Discord bot was implemented on the present artifact-native Playthings runtime. Those are distinct project episodes, and the later design should not be projected backward onto January.

## Generalizable lesson

A graph that changes layout or metaphor should not silently reassign Parent, ownership or evidence. Presentation can change; qualified relationships should not.
