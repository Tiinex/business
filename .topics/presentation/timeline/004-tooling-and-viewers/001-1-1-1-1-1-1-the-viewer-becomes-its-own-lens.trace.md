# The viewer becomes a lens on the records

By **June 18–19**, the site documentation describes `Tiinex Lineage Viewer` as a client-side application that can ingest local material, ZIP bundles, URLs and public GitHub sources, display schema-aware cards and inspect Parent/Origin relations.

- [Original June Lineage Viewer LLM-orientation artifact](https://github.com/Tiinex/site/blob/cc4acebf4a0603ca50bca2bbce375bb3e8d00f77/tiinex.app.llm.v1.md).

Its contract is careful about meaning: a **Parent** means declared continuation; an **Origin** helps ground source/provenance; an external URL mentioned in a body is not automatically a Parent. The viewer is thus an alternate way to inspect the artifacts rather than a replacement for their source meaning.

## Why this belongs next to social outreach

YouTube, Reddit and Discord experiments were source surfaces; the Viewer was an **inspection surface**. Those roles overlap operationally but should remain separate in the narrative.

## Limit

A feature described in a dated orientation document establishes documented intention or state at that revision. It is not automatically evidence that every feature was robust across every browser or host.
