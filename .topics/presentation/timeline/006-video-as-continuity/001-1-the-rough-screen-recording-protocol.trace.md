# Screen recordings became a continuity workaround

During the **July 2026** period, according to the developer's firsthand account and inspection of locally retained recordings, screen captures became a practical way of carrying context between ChatGPT conversations. Rather than rely only on a summary, the developer recorded previous on-screen discussion and supplied the recording to a fresh conversation.

The motivation was a mismatch between the amount of useful conversation context and the friction of moving that context between ChatGPT sessions. The developer also reported account-export delays and no suitable single-chat export workflow available to them at the time.

## What worked and what failed

Video could convey conversational sequence without manually retyping all of it. But it was awkward to inspect, difficult to cite precisely, hard to qualify as typed provenance and a poor repeatable handoff carrier.

## Evidence boundary

This observation comes from firsthand reconstruction and local July recordings examined during presentation preparation, **not** a newly published GitHub artifact or a controlled benchmark. It supports that the workaround was used; it does not prove completeness of the transferred context or define the first moment Handoff ideas appeared.
