# The envelope was a practical convergence

Once the artifacts needed to survive a particular editor or conversation, readers needed a way to distinguish *which schema describes this artifact*, *where its Parent is*, and *whether the current record was the thing to read*.

The **May 28 `tiinex.continuation.v1` document** is a dated example. It specifies `Continuity Context`, `Envelope Schema`, `Parent` and `Current`, with optional `Why` and `Summary` and relation-oriented integrity. It expressly distinguishes an **envelope contract** from the schema of the whole body.

- [May 28 continuity-envelope schema](https://github.com/Tiinex/docs/blob/776fb804109ef51fad8d0f37c630bc32521c1500/.topics/.schemas/tiinex.continuation.v1.md).

## A crucial historical correction

The later `tiinex.root.v1` must not be projected backwards onto May artifacts. The project combined and revised several early conventions over time. Continuity contracts emerging in prose do not imply that every later validation rule was enforced in May.

## What the format bought

The same record could be read in Markdown, rendered by a viewer, held in Git and inspected by tooling — without making any one presentation host the sole semantic authority.
