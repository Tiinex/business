# A practical beginning, not a master plan

The earliest remembered story begins with experimenting with ChatGPT Tasks and weather/radar work. The available results proved unsatisfying, leading to practical implementation experiments rather than a premeditated provenance framework. After a break, a friend asked about a Discord role-playing bot in **January 2026**. The presenter had previously worked on a role-playing intrigue-map interface, which was part of the background to that request.

These were **separate experiments**. Their existence in the personal reconstruction does not make them first-class Tiinex artifacts or prove a direct chain from weather to the eventual schema system.

## Why this chapter exists

It matters that the origins were mundane. A model may retell the project as if it were planned as a standards architecture from day one. The surviving testimony does not support that. The more accurate starting point is practical curiosity and the difficulty of maintaining complex AI-assisted work.

## Evidence status

- **Firsthand retrospective account:** screen-recorded reconstruction and later clarification by the developer, October 2026.
- **Not independently anchored here:** exact timestamps, URLs or repositories for the original weather service, intrigue map and January request.
- **Do not infer:** a formal Tiinex project or any general-purpose provenance architecture existed at this point.
