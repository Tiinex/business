# A practical game request changes the questions

In **January 2026**, according to the developer's firsthand account, a friend asked about making a Discord role-playing game bot. The developer had already explored role-playing interfaces, including an Intrigue Map of characters, relationships and events.

Trying to build this sort of system with AI assistance offered new opportunities to split development work into specialist responsibilities. That is the personal background to the subsequent question of how one specialist's work might be meaningfully reviewed by another.

## Two uses of Discord must not be conflated

1. **January RPG bot request:** a remembered prompt for development work.
2. **May public Tiinex redirect:** a later, separate attempt to give people an entry surface into Tiinex. Its source and limits are examined in epoch 004.

The two are chronologically distinct. The available evidence does not establish that the friend-request bot and the later public Discord entry channel were one project.

## Evidence boundary

The request date and personal motivation are firsthand retrospective testimony. The later redirect has a repository revision. No claims are made here about bot deployment, membership numbers or conversation ingestion.
