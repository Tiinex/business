# Tiinex: from practical experiments to inspectable continuity

Tiinex is an evolving system for **portable, inspectable artifacts and qualified continuity** in AI-assisted work. It attempts to keep important working state outside a single opaque conversation and to make a recipient inspect the relationships, evidence, authority boundaries and tasks that survived a transition.

This presentation is **not** a normative specification, an interoperability certification or a claim that the system is production-complete. It is a public-facing explanation of how a collection of practical experiments converged on today's design.

## What a reader can explore

- **Timeline:** nine chapters presenting the history in a readable sequence, each with evidence and explicit uncertainty.
- **Source anchors:** commit-pinned permalinks point **to the original files or artifacts**, not merely to a commit listing.
- **Demonstrable mechanism:** Markdown artifacts carry typed schema identity, continuity metadata and links; Tooling can qualify, inspect and package the relevant material.
- **Review boundary:** copying a carrier, validating a digest, recovering a previous state and making a justified claim are different assertions.

## Why this is a presentation lineage, not historical causality

The chapter Parent links were deliberately authored during this presentation work. They express an **editorial reading sequence**. They do not claim that the original weather experiment, role-playing request, agent tool, viewer or Handoff Package formed this exact Parent chain when they occurred. Their original dates and relationships must be read from the linked primary evidence; recollections remain marked as such.

## How to navigate

Start with the [reader guide](001-1-reader-guide.trace.md), then open chapter 001 under `timeline/` and follow each **Parent** back from chapter 009 to the presentation Topic and the actual Tiinex Organization artifact. The displayed order is reader-friendly chronological/thematic order, and the Parent links run in the reverse direction. A chapter may refer to work from an earlier stage when an idea was revisited later.

## What this does not establish

- A correct checksum does not mean the prose is true.
- A qualified Handoff does not automatically grant someone authority to act.
- A working demo in one host is not proof of all-host portability.
- A narrated date without a primary source is still a recollection.
