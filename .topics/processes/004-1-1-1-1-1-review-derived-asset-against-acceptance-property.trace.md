# Review Derived Asset Against Acceptance Property

## Current Read

Mechanical validation and human acceptance are separate. The review surface must expose the property being judged: temporal playback may help cadence review, an ordered contact/sequence may better expose pose progression, a zoomed still may expose edge quality, and another domain may require a different representation.

A single review medium must not be treated as sufficient merely because it is easy to render.

## Design Direction

Declare the acceptance property, primary review representation, secondary review representations when useful, human acceptance authority, and return path on rejection. Release or downstream use begins only after the applicable review/qualification boundary is satisfied.

## Next Artifacts

- Domain-specific process specializations and execution lineages may continue from their own organizational parent while referencing this process as reusable guidance.
