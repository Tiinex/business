# Adopt Scoped Specialist Carriage for Refactor Turn 2

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: specialist Handoff Workspace scope during Refactor Turn 2
- Decision: use Foundation Scoped Specialist Carriage for delegated Refactor work. Child roles receive the minimum sufficient set of complete Workspace sources and required authority/contract context; the Refactor Anchor retains and reconciles the full current multi-workspace frontier. This supersedes the older fixed Business+Docs+Site carriage requirement only for this bounded Turn-2 delegation context.

## Basis

- Current Refactor work spans more independent repositories than the historical 3/3 Foundation shape.
- Giving every child the entire frontier increases grounding cost and authority confusion without improving source truth.
- Full-source integrity is preserved because every Workspace actually carried remains complete and exactly bound.
- Anchor reconciliation, not child package breadth, protects current-source continuity.

## Consequences

- Child Handoffs must state Allowed Scope, Out of Scope, exact source inputs and return/blocker conditions.
- Older sibling Workspaces in a return never overwrite newer Anchor current source automatically.
- Anchor-to-Sigma canonical delivery remains free to carry the full current frontier when that is the delivery scope.
- The broader Cross-Repository Work Turn remains operative except where this bounded decision explicitly changes specialist carriage breadth.

## Review Conditions

Review after Turn 2 or if scoped carriers repeatedly fail cold-start/grounding sufficiency.
