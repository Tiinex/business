# Conversation Successor Handoff

## Purpose

Move a long-running role to a fresh conversation before conversation/context degradation becomes an operational risk.

## Signals

Signals may include repeated successor branches reported by the operator, approaching known context/runtime limits, prior recovery dependence, or increasing reliance on chat-only assumptions. UI/chat naming is only an operational heuristic and never Tiinex lineage authority.

## Procedure

- Recommend successor Handoff before actual context failure when practical.
- Finish the current safe tranche where reasonable and ensure valuable work is durable.
- Manufacture a normal role-to-role Handoff carrying the minimum sufficient complete Workspace source for its qualified scope.
- Preserve controlling semantic/work lineage when the objective remains the same; carrier progression may advance independently.
- New chat/session identity does not itself advance Parent lineage, filename allocation, carrier major or package version.
- Successor performs cold-start and grounding verification before mutation.

## Grounding Gap Review

Use the successor transition as a test of durable grounding. Recurrent operator convention belongs in Process; role-specific responsibility belongs in Role; semantic ambiguity goes to canonical semantic authority; Tooling/allocation ambiguity goes to the qualified Tooling/contract surface.
