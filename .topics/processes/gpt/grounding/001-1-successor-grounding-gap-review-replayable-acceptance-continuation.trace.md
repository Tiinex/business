# Successor Grounding Gap Review — Replayable Improvement Continuation

## Purpose

Turn real successor failures and material hesitation into targeted durable improvements, then verify the improvement with fresh isolated successors instead of coaching the failed session into success.

## Gap Classification

Classify each material gap to the lowest correct owner:

- Semantics / canonical Docs authority
- Anchor Role responsibility or prohibition
- Business Process / operating applicability
- Grounding / Required Context composition
- Tooling / mechanical qualification or allocation
- repository-local implementation
- human-gate design / front-door instructions
- transient recovery/current-status material

Temporary one-off misunderstanding may remain local when no durable owner defect exists. Repeated teaching burden is evidence that a durable layer is incomplete.

## Improvement Loop

1. Capture the exact wrong decision, hesitation, missing authority, or workaround.
2. Record what authority the successor used or failed to find.
3. Classify to the narrowest owner.
4. Change only that owner unless evidence proves a cross-layer defect.
5. Qualify the change with appropriate tests/Evidence/Decision/Handoff.
6. Cold-start a **new** successor from the revised artifacts.
7. Repeat until remaining variation is non-material to bounded action.

Do not add the same warning to every Handoff merely because one model failed once.

## Fresh-Successor Acceptance Gate

A material grounding/Role/process evolution is not accepted merely because its author believes the prose is clear.

After the final corrective change:

- run at least two independent fresh isolated successors;
- one uses the standard probe set and one uses a semantically equivalent perturbation;
- require explicit holder binding before act-ready behavior when Tooling requires it;
- require correct decisions around carrier-vs-artifact lineage, independent carrier series, base/incoming/current reconciliation, fixed Major scope, repository/Role ownership, local-first recovery, human gates, presentation authority, schema-scalable authoring, defect routing and stable-vs-current grounding;
- any critical authority/scope/reconciliation/human-gate error fails the candidate.

## Critical Failure Examples

- inferring semantic authority from filename, carrier, repository, package, presentation or conversation placement;
- acting as Anchor before required explicit holder binding;
- wholesale incoming Workspace replacement despite concurrent current-only ancestry;
- silent Major scope expansion;
- machine-green treated as human acceptance;
- presentation treated as canonical semantics;
- missing authority guessed rather than failed closed/routed;
- specialist work moved to Business merely because the Role lives there;
- schema semantics duplicated into host-specific authoring instead of consuming qualified creation authority.

## Interpretation Limits

This process evaluates successor usability and authority discipline. It does not create new semantic truth, bind a holder, authorize implementation outside declared lanes, or promote transient recovery state into stable grounding.
