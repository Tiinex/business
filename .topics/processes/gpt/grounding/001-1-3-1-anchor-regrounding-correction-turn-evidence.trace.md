# Anchor Re-Grounding Correction-Turn Evidence

## Supported Claim Or Question

- Supported Claim Or Question: whether recent Anchor grounding evolution materially reduced the amount of corrective Sigma teaching required before a fresh Anchor can make bounded Tiinex decisions
- Evidence Role: bounded retained evidence for adopting correction-turn count as the primary practical re-grounding quality metric and for making two-phase stable/current grounding an explicit Anchor duty

## Provenance

- Known Source: replayable fresh-Anchor standard/perturbed successor evidence, retained Anchor grading artifacts, current Executive Grounding/Anchor Role/process lineage, and Sigma's explicit clarification that re-grounding speed means the number of corrective information turns he must supply
- Preservation Basis: the cited fresh-successor artifacts remain durable Business evidence and distinguish decision quality from strict-isolation harness quality
- Provenance Limits: this is not a wall-clock benchmark and does not claim every future model/session will behave identically

## Evidence Material

- Material: the perturbed fresh-Anchor successor completed the bounded authority/reconciliation/human-gate decision set without material Sigma coaching; later coverage-corrected standard and perturbed runs made the required stable distinctions materially correctly, while the remaining failures concerned isolation of the test harness rather than foundational Anchor decisions. Across those qualified decision runs, the observed correction-turn requirement for the stable categories was effectively zero. Earlier cold-start work repeatedly required explicit corrections around holder binding, carrier-vs-artifact lineage, repository/Role ownership, package route assumptions and human-gate boundaries. The improvement therefore appears primarily as fewer corrective operator turns rather than lower Tooling wall-clock latency.
- Material Kind: successor-grounding operational evidence

## Preservation And Fidelity

- Preservation State: current fresh-successor evidence is not rewritten; this artifact summarizes the practical operator metric newly clarified by Sigma
- Fidelity Notes: strict clean-isolation acceptance remained a separate test-harness concern and is not represented as a failure of the twelve-category decision model
- Known Losses: no statistically meaningful sample across many model/provider families exists yet; the next real Anchor takeover is the next production dogfood observation

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: zero future correction turns in every session, faster CLI execution, permanent model competence, or permission to ignore new Sigma requirements
- Not Yet Used As: universal performance benchmark or release criterion outside Anchor successor grounding
- Must Not Be Treated As: evidence that human input is unnecessary; Sigma remains authoritative for new intent, product judgment and explicit human gates
