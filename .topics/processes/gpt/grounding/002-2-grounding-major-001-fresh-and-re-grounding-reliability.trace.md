# Grounding Major 001 — Fresh And Re-Grounding Reliability

## Objective

Turn the currently observed grounding/process regressions into a durable, replayable operating improvement so fresh and re-grounded Roles recover the right mission, authority, program context, recipient behavior, validation discipline and transport convention without Sigma reteaching them or acting as debugger/patch applier.

## Why This Major Exists

- Fresh Anchor grounding improved only after explicit Sigma correction to use Tooling-backed stable self-grounding and broader program recovery.
- Fresh Kodax sessions were locally competent but repeatedly returned work before coarse workspace health was established, causing Anchor to become downstream QA and Sigma to discover ordinary integration/build regressions.
- One Kodax iteration broke the established qualified-carrier transport convention by asking Sigma to apply a loose Git patch.
- These are not isolated feature defects. They indicate that role/process grounding is not yet reliably converting durable Tiinex material into the expected operating behavior.
- ChatGPT sessions are disposable and can hit hard turn/branch/runtime limits. Human correction turns therefore create compounding cost and recovery risk.

## Done Criteria

- A fresh Anchor successor, given only the qualified carrier and normal routing text, naturally performs stable organization/Role/program grounding before allowing the local frontier to dominate attention, without Sigma prompting it to use Tooling or widen its grounding.
- Re-grounding triggers are durable and actionable: material specialist return, new Recovery, Major open/close, reconciliation, authority uncertainty, material context drift and branch/fork continuation all cause the relevant program view to be reassessed before further mutation.
- Recipient projection is operational rather than merely understood: Sigma-facing output defaults to scan-first meaningful delta, blocker and next human action; Role/Prefix/Workspace remain distinct; copyable routing stays exact.
- Specialist Roles recover and act on epistemic ownership: implementation, ordinary compile/build/broad health evidence and explicit blockers remain with the specialist. Focused tests support rather than replace coarse health gates.
- Qualified Handoff package plus exact routing text remains the normal completion transport. Loose patches/manual source application by Sigma are treated as a process failure, not a convenience path.
- The Major identifies which improvements belong in Business/Role/Process, Docs/Axiom, Core/Loom or host-specific surfaces and routes them to the narrowest correct owner rather than accumulating private Anchor workarounds.
- At least one fresh replay is performed from the durable result with no coaching. Correction turns are recorded as evidence; success requires the successor to recover the intended behavior from project-owned material rather than parent-Anchor prose.
- Produce one stable, committable Recovery checkpoint after accepted changes so the next fresh Anchor can recover this Major's result without chat memory.

## Scope

Business/Anchor grounding and re-grounding process, reusable Role operating expectations, recipient projection, qualified-return/transport discipline, coarse validation expectations, fresh-successor replay and the routing of any discovered Tooling/schema/host gaps to their owning lanes.

This Major may author bounded Business artifacts and may create child Tasks/Handoffs for Axiom, Loom or host specialists when evidence shows a durable change belongs there. It does not itself absorb those peer authorities.

## Dependencies

- [Anchor Grounding, Recovery And Recipient Projection Hardening](002-anchor-grounding-recovery-and-recipient-projection-hardening.trace.md)
- [Specialist Epistemic Ownership And Qualified Return Discipline](002-1-specialist-epistemic-ownership-and-qualified-return-discipline.trace.md)
- Current qualified Anchor, Sigma, Kodax, Axiom and Loom Role artifacts.
- Current full Anchor Recovery carrying the last accepted program frontier.
- Live VS Code Major 003 evidence as process evidence only; unaccepted VS Code candidate bytes are not part of this Major's accepted source frontier.

## Exclusions

- Do not redefine `grounded-to-act` into a global project-understanding claim.
- Do not invent new canonical grounding states merely for explanation convenience.
- Do not hardcode ChatGPT nicknames, chat titles, Sigma identity or provider-specific UI as Role authority.
- Do not create a Sub-Anchor program-segment hierarchy as part of this Major; this lane is a bounded successor/process hardening experiment under Master Anchor.
- Do not broaden into unrelated Tiinex product work, release/publication, remote mutation or unqualified repo changes.
- Do not solve specialist validation weakness by adding one bespoke regression test per symptom when an existing coarse repository-health gate should catch the defect class.

## Measurement

- Count fresh/re-grounding correction turns required from Sigma.
- Count cases where Sigma is asked to debug, patch, repair source or discover ordinary build failure.
- Count transport-convention violations.
- Record whether fresh successors recover the intended scan-first communication and program context without parent-chat coaching.
- Treat lower human correction/rework and preserved exact-byte recovery as the success signal, not raw artifact/test count.

## Return Boundary

Return to Master Anchor with: durable changes made, owner-routed child work if any, replay evidence, unresolved blockers, correction-turn count, and a qualified Recovery/continuation path. The Master Anchor retains cross-program integration, final audit and Sigma-gate authority.
