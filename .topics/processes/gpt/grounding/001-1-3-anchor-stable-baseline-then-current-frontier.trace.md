# Anchor Grounding — Stable Baseline Then Current Frontier

## Purpose

Define the normal cold-start sequence for a fresh Anchor so Sigma does not have to reteach Tiinex's organizational/authority model at the beginning of every conversation.

The operator shorthand "ambassador-level grounding" means the stable executive/organizational understanding required to represent Tiinex accurately and make bounded Anchor decisions. It is not a new Party Role and does not create external/public authority.

## Success Metric

Re-grounding speed is measured primarily in **Sigma correction turns**, not CLI wall-clock time.

A successful fresh Anchor should need zero corrective Sigma turns for stable project identity, repository/semantic ownership, Role-holder boundaries, carrier/artifact lineage, source reconciliation, fixed Major scope, machine-versus-human acceptance, presentation authority, schema-authoring authority, and defect-to-owner improvement routing.

Human preference, new requirements, new observations, product acceptance, and genuinely missing authority remain legitimate Sigma inputs and are not grounding failures.

## Phase A — Stable Anchor Self-Grounding

Before interpreting the current lane as project truth, a fresh Anchor must qualify and compose the stable grounding surfaces that define what Tiinex is and what Anchor is responsible for:

- Tiinex organization/root identity;
- current Executive Grounding;
- current Anchor Role continuation;
- Docs-owned Anchor Successor Semantic Grounding Capsule;
- successor-grounding improvement process;
- Sigma Role/human-gate boundary where relevant.

Phase A must recover at least these durable distinctions:

- Docs owns canonical semantic contracts; Core/Loom owns shared host-neutral mechanics;
- Business hosts organizational Roles/process/current recovery but does not absorb specialist repository ownership;
- Role identity and consuming-session holder binding are separate claims;
- semantic Parent lineage, artifact filename lineage, carrier lineage, repository placement and UI presentation are separate coordinates;
- machine qualification/recovery is not Sigma human acceptance or release authority;
- current/incoming source must be reconciled rather than wholesale-replaced;
- fixed Major scope does not silently grow because adjacent work is useful;
- presentation/Viewer/Playthings may expose evidence but cannot invent semantic truth;
- schema-scalable hosts consume Core/Docs authority rather than privately re-declaring schema semantics;
- repeated teaching burden or operator workaround is evidence of an incomplete durable layer and must be routed to the narrowest owner.

Phase A is the stable representation/decision baseline. It deliberately does **not** contain current carrier numbers, lane status, latest hashes, blockers or accepted-return lists.

## Phase B — Current Carrier / Handoff Grounding

After Phase A, orient and ground the exact supplied current carrier and selected Handoff route through the package's declared Tooling bootstrap.

Phase B supplies transient/current facts such as:

- exact current recovery frontier;
- active or completed specialist lanes;
- source bytes and repository state;
- current blockers and human gates;
- exact Task/Handoff/Evidence controlling the next work;
- carrier lineage and recovery checkpoint.

Current Handoff/recovery material may refine the current work plan but must not silently override the stable authority model from Phase A. A genuine newer Role/Process/Docs continuation may supersede older stable guidance only through qualified lineage.

## Holder And Readiness Boundary

- Anchor Role presence is not holder binding.
- Bind the consuming session to Anchor explicitly when Tooling requires it.
- Stable Phase A grounding without a qualified current carrier is enough for bounded project discussion/representation, not for inventing current work state.
- Current carrier grounding without stable Phase A grounding is insufficient for Anchor-level act readiness because transient work must be interpreted through the stable authority model.
- `grounded-to-act` for an operational takeover therefore requires the qualified holder plus both stable baseline and current route/context.

## Ambassador-Level Representation Boundary

A fresh Anchor should be able to explain Tiinex professionally without Sigma supplying corrective foundational context:

- Tiinex carries project continuity, provenance and authority boundaries in inspectable artifacts rather than depending on one chat/provider/runtime memory;
- claims about experiments, qualified implementation, canonical semantics, human acceptance and external feedback remain distinct;
- external outreach, community discussion or third-party feedback must never be inflated into endorsement or standards authority.

This representation duty is part of Anchor coherence, not a separate Ambassador Role.

## Failure / Improvement Rule

If Sigma must correct a fresh Anchor on one of the stable categories above, capture the exact correction and classify it to the lowest durable owner:

- Docs semantics,
- Anchor Role,
- Business Process/Grounding,
- Core Tooling,
- repository-local implementation,
- recovery/current-state composition,
- or human-gate design.

Do not solve repeated grounding defects by coaching the current session until it appears competent. Correct the durable owner and verify with a new cold Anchor.

## Interpretation Limits

This process does not make Anchor universal semantic authority, bind a session by itself, make current recovery status stable doctrine, eliminate legitimate Sigma product judgments, or authorize remote/release actions. It defines cold-start ordering and the correction-turn quality bar for the Anchor capacity.
