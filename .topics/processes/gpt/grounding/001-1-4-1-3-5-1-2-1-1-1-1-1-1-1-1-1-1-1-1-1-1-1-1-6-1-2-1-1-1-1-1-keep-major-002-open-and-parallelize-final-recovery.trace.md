## Decision

- Subject: whether the latest Sigma replay is sufficient for Major 002 closure or requires another bounded root-cause recovery before real-host acceptance
- Decision: keep Major 002 open and run one parallel Loom/Core + Kodax/VS Code recovery against the latest exact Sigma evidence. Preserve the now-working primary single-Handoff path while closing carrier purity, multi-route Transport, live Workspace discovery and host lifecycle defects before another Sigma gate.

## Basis

- The latest real replay proves meaningful progress: a fresh Outgoing can reach successful Pack and Transport with Role closure intact.
- The exact generated carrier still exposes `tiinex-recipient-v2.transport.json` at recipient-facing root even though receiver semantic authority does not belong in that compatibility surface and Core already contains a clean-carrier Phase 2 implementation.

- Anchor retains fan-in, cross-layer acceptance and Sigma sequencing.
- Axiom remains dormant unless a specialist returns an exact canonical semantic contradiction.

## Review Conditions
