## Exclusions And Dependencies

- no-parent-chat-coaching
  - Kind: excluded-scope
  - Description: do not supply completed replay answers or ad hoc explanatory coaching as replay input; recover behavior from qualified durable material and normal routing only.

- no-source-mutation
  - Kind: excluded-scope
  - Description: the replay itself performs no product/schema/Tooling/source mutation or remote action.

- independent-successor-boundary
  - Kind: unresolved-dependency
  - Description: Tiinex can qualify the cold-start ingress mechanically, but independent successor cognitive/communication behavior requires a genuinely fresh model/session and must remain open if this runtime cannot spawn one.
