# Foundation Cross-Repository Work Turn Adoption

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: current Foundation cross-repository collaboration process
- Decision: adopt [Cross-Repository Work Turn](002-cross-repository-work-turn-process.trace.md) as the current operative collaboration guidance for Tiinex Foundation work across Business, Docs, Site, Anchor, Axiom, Loom, and Sigma where that process is explicitly in scope. This adoption is bounded to Foundation collaboration and does not make the process universal Tiinex policy.

## Basis

- The process artifact already reflects the repeated real work loop: stable human checkpoints, full-source inter-role carriage, role-bounded specialist work, return reconciliation, post-landing reduction, and evidence-driven workflow improvement.
- Sigma has repeatedly asked that actual collaboration shape be made durable and may be revised or decomposed when reality differs from the represented process.
- Axiom's composable operational-grounding discovery requires operative guidance to be explicitly adopted rather than silently inferred from process prose, package membership, or chat memory.

## Consequences

- Handoffs that require this operating guidance should carry this Decision and the exact process artifact as Required Context.
- Applicability to the Foundation Readiness context is materialized separately as an explicit typed Relation child of this Decision.
- Revisions to the process remain allowed when actual collaboration provides better evidence; material changes to operative scope or effect require a corresponding Decision update or successor rather than silent authority drift.
- The process remains a Topic representation; this Decision does not create a generic Policy schema or settle whether a dedicated Process schema is later useful.
- Human acceptance, repository landing, and technical qualification remain owned by their existing gates and are not implied by this adoption.

## Review Conditions

- Review when Foundation Readiness closes, when the work-turn model is proposed for non-Foundation use, or when repeated process lifecycle needs justify a dedicated Process schema evaluation.
