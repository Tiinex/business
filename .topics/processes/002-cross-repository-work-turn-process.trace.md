# Cross-Repository Work Turn

## Current Read

This process projects the real Foundation work loop observed across Business, Docs, and Site. It is an operating-process representation, not a new schema or claim that every future Tiinex project must use the same cadence.

## Turn Shape

- Establish or close the current Business work frontier before opening a new cross-repository specialist turn.
- When a new stable Business-grounded turn will create new specialist descendants in Docs, Site, or another repository, start a new local Business major first. The major is a human stable-point coordinate; semantic continuity remains declared by Parent and may cross the major boundary.
- Before specialist work begins from that new major, manufacture a full-source carrier containing complete carried Business, Docs, and Site source and return it to Sigma for the human commit/push transport checkpoint. Do not silently replace a lost carried Workspace with a fresh remote checkout and treat it as continuity-equivalent.
- After the Business checkpoint is landed, open bounded specialist Handoffs. Specialist semantic/implementation lineage lives in its natural repository; Business retains organizational why, priority, acceptance boundary, and cross-role disposition rather than mirroring technical detail.
- Inside a specialist repository, filename dimension follows the real local Parent progression. Serial Handoff -> research/discovery/implementation -> return steps extend the same branch; role/session/transport boundaries are not major boundaries. A new local major requires an explicit stable/re-anchor reason, while genuinely parallel current work may remain distinct branches.
- Before accepting a role return into source, separate semantic/source changes from generated Tooling output. Raw validation/checkpoint receipts such as transient JSON are build/package evidence by default and must not enter the current `.topics` source surface merely because they were produced during qualification. If evidence deserves durable source provenance, promote or summarize it in an appropriate semantic artifact; package/build-local raw receipts may remain transport evidence.
- One full-source carrier may serve multiple roles when canonical Tooling supports multiple qualified routes; recipient transport text selects the exact route without requiring duplicate ZIP carriers.
- Role returns should use canonical Handoff manufacture and preserve the inherited full-source carrier during Foundation even when work scope is narrow. A successful work result does not excuse a transport-convention regression.
- Anchor reconciles returns, preserves Sigma observations as explicit human evidence when material, and starts another Business major before opening a materially new cross-repository turn.

## Improvement Loop

- If real workflow repeatedly differs from the represented process, treat the mismatch as evidence rather than human folklore.
- Missing subprocess, role blind spot, transport convention drift, repeated manual intervention, or recurring LLM teaching burden should be routed to the appropriate Business process/role decision, Axiom semantic authority, or Loom Tooling improvement.
- Process/role improvements must preserve existing authority boundaries: Anchor coordinates and reconciles, Axiom owns canonical schema semantics, Loom owns shared Tooling implementation, and Sigma owns human observation/acceptance signals where relevant.

## Carrier Convenience

- Carrier lineage is a consumer-facing progress/retry/stability projection, not source Parent authority.
- If no earlier carrier major is known, export may begin at major `001` without repository-global carrier-history reconstruction.
- A known stable carrier chain may bump major at explicit stable checkpoints; turns/retries/divergence may use deeper suffixes.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Mean: every artifact must be shipped to every product consumer; full-source carriage is currently a Foundation collaboration safety invariant, while product-level scoped export has its own bounded Workspace Representation semantics.
- Must Not Be Used To Claim: remote publication, Sigma acceptance merely because a package exists, permission to bypass host safeguards, or authority for a role to implement outside its lane.
