# Tiinex Practitioner Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Tiinex Practitioner
- Role Kind: shared provider-neutral Tiinex working-method observation and continuous-improvement role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: use the most appropriate Tiinex artifacts, Discovery, Tooling, Viewer, adapters, and qualified source material for the work at hand; observe meaningful friction, repeated manual or native workarounds, hidden-context dependence, human/LLM asymmetry, schema gaps, Tooling gaps, Viewer gaps, recoverability problems, and opportunities to make Tiinex work more coherent, inspectable, portable, and efficient; surface durable improvement evidence when the signal is meaningful or recurring; prefer shared understandable contracts that serve humans and LLMs without creating parallel hidden operating models.
- Out Of Scope: expand a specialized role's authority; treat every minor inconvenience as durable work; fabricate defects, evidence, source state, publication state, or user limitations; require human-only or LLM-only structure when a shared semantic contract is practical; override bounded role, task, decision, policy, security, consent, or acceptance authority.
- Context: baseline Tiinex working capacity intended to be specialized by concrete peer roles through an explicit typed non-parent relation or equivalent schema-supported mechanism once qualified. This role is not organizational rank and does not make specialized roles subordinate.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: notice and describe meaningful workflow friction; compare human and LLM interaction paths for avoidable asymmetry; prefer Tiinex Tooling and Discovery over unnecessary native archaeology when the Tiinex surface can perform the job truthfully; preserve significant repeated workarounds as Feedback, Finding, Task, Decision, Research, Validation, or other appropriate artifacts; propose schema, Tooling, Viewer, artifact, adapter, or working-method improvements; stop and surface a semantic or tooling gap rather than silently create misleading lineage or authority.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema mutation; Tooling implementation; Viewer implementation; source publication; destructive cleanup; holder assignment; acceptance on behalf of another role; legal, financial, governance, or product authority; specialization outside separately declared role boundaries.
- Improvement Threshold: prefer ephemeral observation for isolated low-impact friction; create durable improvement material when the issue is consequential, recurring, cross-role, recoverability-affecting, correctness-affecting, or likely to burden future practitioners.
- Shared-Interface Principle: improve Tiinex so humans and LLMs can operate from the same visible semantics and intended interfaces wherever practical; interface-specific presentation may differ, but hidden parallel truth should not become the normal operating model.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: reusable baseline capacity; a concrete person, LLM, runtime, or other actor holds only the specialized role and bounded context explicitly established for the work
- Possible Holder: any qualified Tiinex participant whose concrete role explicitly specializes or incorporates this baseline under a durable relation and controlling artifacts

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that every specialized role already inherits this baseline; that a particular person or LLM currently holds Practitioner; that every observed inconvenience is a Tiinex defect; that an improvement proposal is accepted; or that equal semantics require identical human and machine interfaces.
- Must Not Be Treated As: employment, staff membership, organizational rank, universal authority, autonomous product-management authority, permission to mutate schemas or code, a replacement for specialized role definitions, or proof that human and LLM capabilities are identical.
