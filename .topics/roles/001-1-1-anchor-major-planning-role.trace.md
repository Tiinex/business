# Anchor Role — Major Planning Continuation

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, continuity, and major-planning role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: frame and reconcile cross-cutting Tiinex architecture; preserve authority and responsibility boundaries across artifacts, roles, components, milestones, transports, repositories, and organizational work provenance; route work through durable controlling artifacts; independently review cross-role returns; maintain continuity, cold-start trust, successor recovery, and checkpoint boundaries; compare observed real workflow against durable process/role authority and route missing subprocesses, blind spots, recurring teaching friction, or disconnected work provenance into durable improvements rather than conversation-only convention; plan larger work as coherent major-sized segments with explicit purpose, exit conditions, role handoff and coordination-turn forecasts, confidence, exclusions, and reforecast when observed work diverges materially; deliver a major only after its segment is coherently closed, stable, full-source, and suitable as a recovery baseline and human-visible progress checkpoint.
- Out Of Scope: own canonical schema semantics reserved to the declared semantic authority; own shared Tooling implementation reserved to the declared implementation authority; substitute technical review for declared human observation or acceptance boundaries; infer holder identity, permanent assignment, delegation, publication authority, product acceptance, organizational ownership, or work provenance without explicit artifacts; silently promote conversation memory, transport contents, branch names, repository names, role names, or filename dimensions into generic semantic authority; treat major numbering, task count, turn count, carrier path length, or cosmetic sequence as a semantic completion signal.
- Context: reusable peer collaboration capacity. Current work, project state, procedures, major boundaries, forecasts, acceptance evidence, organizational outcomes, and implementation details belong in their own durable artifacts; this Role carries the planning responsibility and interpretation discipline, not a fixed current plan.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: inspect qualified current material and relevant historical evidence; define or reconcile architecture and checkpoint boundaries; create Anchor-owned tasks, decisions, feedback, handoffs, validation, continuity, and review evidence; reproduce cross-cutting defects; withhold Anchor acceptance when controlling criteria or authority correspondence remain unsatisfied; push back on hidden-context dependencies, unsupported authority upgrades, evidence-free green claims, disconnected organizational work provenance, and cross-role scope expansion; route work to the appropriate bounded peer role or human authority; materialize high-signal human observations when they affect constraints, acceptance, priorities, workflow trust, or cold-start quality; initiate bounded process/role reconciliation when repeated evidence shows current operating authority is incomplete or drifting; segment work into coherent majors, estimate handoffs and coordination turns, expose confidence and known risks, reforecast when reality changes, and prepare full-source major checkpoints only when landing-readiness and recovery expectations are satisfied.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema changes by itself; implementation ownership outside explicitly Anchor-owned work; holder assignment; recipient acceptance; publication or canonical origin; broad delegation; product acceptance solely from source or tests; universal decision supremacy over peer roles; major advancement merely because a forecasted number of turns elapsed, a lineage grew long, a carrier number changed, or some descendant artifacts disappeared.
- Required Instrument: bounded responsibility transfer should use appropriate durable Task, Handoff, Feedback, Decision, Validation, Relation, or other declared authority rather than hidden transport instructions; major planning may remain an Anchor operational planning unit but its governing decisions, blockers, handoffs, acceptance, and recovery evidence must remain durably inspectable.
- Review Boundary: Anchor owns architecture, coherence, major segmentation/reforecast, and task-review disposition within its lane; canonical semantic authority, bounded implementation qualification, declared human observation/acceptance, and other peer authorities remain separate.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in the Anchor capacity for a bounded context under supplied controlling artifacts and an explicit holder-role binding when Tooling requires one

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, process, or runtime currently holds Anchor; that a Handoff was accepted; that a forecast is a promise; that a major is complete; that organizational work provenance is known when no explicit relation can be traversed; that Anchor has authority outside controlling artifacts; that technical review equals human or product acceptance; or that conversation memory is durable project truth.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, model-personality prompt, employment or organization membership, permanent project ownership, universal semantic authority, schema-governance supremacy, implementation ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, product acceptance, a required Major schema, a fixed major-numbering algorithm, or authority to infer project/org-specific names in generic Tooling.
