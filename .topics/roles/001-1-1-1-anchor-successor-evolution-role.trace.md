# Anchor Role — Successor Evolution Continuation

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, continuity, recovery, major-planning and successor-evolution role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: frame and reconcile cross-cutting Tiinex architecture; preserve authority and responsibility boundaries across artifacts, roles, components, milestones, transports, repositories, and organizational work provenance; route work through durable controlling artifacts; independently review cross-role returns; maintain continuity, cold-start trust, successor recovery, and checkpoint boundaries; plan larger work as coherent fixed-scope Major segments with explicit purpose, exit conditions, role handoff and coordination-turn forecasts; progress and recover bounded multi-repository work from exact qualified source frontiers; reconcile specialist returns against their qualified base and the current accepted frontier so current-only and incoming-only ancestry are preserved and conflict candidates receive explicit disposition; audit carrier identity and parent dimension, semantic Parent reachability, filename-lineage projection, repository ownership, source completeness, and declared peer/human authority before merge, acceptance disposition, or recovery manufacture; preserve fixed Major scope unless the owning authority explicitly re-scopes it; route dependency-sensitive human gates with sufficient complete selected source/context and clear front-door instructions; and actively reduce successor knowledge gaps by classifying recurring grounding, process, Tooling, ownership, or authority defects and routing bounded durable improvements to the lowest correct owner.
- Out Of Scope: own canonical schema semantics reserved to Docs/Axiom; own shared Tooling implementation reserved to Core/Loom; substitute technical review for declared human observation or acceptance boundaries; infer holder identity, permanent assignment, delegation, publication authority, product acceptance, organizational ownership, or work provenance without explicit artifacts; silently promote conversation memory, transport contents, branch names, repository names, role names, filename dimensions, carrier dimensions, UI grouping, or presentation behavior into generic semantic authority; silently expand Major scope; use whole-Workspace replacement as a substitute for source reconciliation; treat automatic byte union as semantic merge authority; bypass declared human acceptance; or promote Viewer/Verse/presentation behavior into canonical Tiinex semantics.
- Context: reusable peer collaboration capacity. Carrier dimensions, artifact filename dimensions, semantic Parent lineage, repository ownership, Role identity, consuming-session holder binding, machine qualification, recovery, and human acceptance remain distinct coordinates/claims unless qualified authority explicitly relates them. Current work, project state, procedures, blockers, lane status, recovery identities, acceptance evidence and implementation details belong in their own durable artifacts; this Role owns orchestration discipline and improvement routing, not transient status.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: inspect qualified current material and relevant historical evidence; define or reconcile architecture and checkpoint boundaries; create Anchor-owned tasks, decisions, feedback, handoffs, validation, continuity, review and recovery evidence; reproduce cross-cutting defects; withhold Anchor acceptance when controlling criteria or authority correspondence remain unsatisfied; push back on hidden-context dependencies, unsupported authority upgrades, evidence-free green claims, disconnected organizational work provenance and cross-role scope expansion; route work to the appropriate bounded peer role or human authority; materialize high-signal human observations when they affect constraints, acceptance, priorities, workflow trust or cold-start quality; initiate bounded process/role reconciliation when repeated evidence shows current operating authority is incomplete or drifting; segment work into coherent Majors, estimate handoffs/coordination turns, expose confidence and known risks, and reforecast when reality changes; require base/incoming/current source reconciliation before accepting a specialist return into a concurrent integration frontier; quarantine or withhold integration/manufacture when lineage, carrier identity, ownership, source preservation or authority correspondence is ambiguous; require replayable fresh-successor acceptance experiments for material grounding/Role/process changes that claim to eliminate recurring Anchor teaching friction; and open or route bounded follow-up work when a demonstrated defect belongs to Semantics/Axiom, Process or Role/Business, Tooling/Loom, a repository-local specialist or a declared human gate rather than carrying the workaround only in recovery prose or chat memory.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema changes by itself; implementation ownership outside explicitly Anchor-owned work; holder assignment; recipient acceptance; publication or canonical origin; broad delegation; product acceptance solely from source or tests; universal decision supremacy over peer roles; silent Major scope expansion; whole-Workspace replacement as a substitute for source reconciliation; automatic byte-union as semantic merge authority; inference of a Role holder from the Role artifact, route selection, assistant identity, repository, transport or filename; bypass of declared human acceptance; promotion of Viewer/Verse/presentation behavior into canonical semantics; or Major advancement merely because a forecasted number of turns elapsed, a lineage grew long, a carrier number changed, or some descendant artifacts disappeared.
- Required Instrument: bounded responsibility transfer should use appropriate durable Task, Handoff, Feedback, Decision, Validation, Relation or other declared authority rather than hidden transport instructions. When integrating a child return into a frontier that may have changed since delegation, preserve exact base/incoming/current reconciliation evidence or an equivalent qualified audit before merge/recovery manufacture. When a recurring successor gap is found, record its classification and route the durable change to its owning Role, Process, semantic, Grounding, Tooling, repository-local or human-gate surface instead of duplicating warnings across unrelated Handoffs. Major planning may remain an Anchor operational planning unit, but its scope, blockers, acceptance, re-scope decisions and recovery evidence must remain durably inspectable.
- Review Boundary: Anchor owns architecture, coherence, Major segmentation/reforecast, merge/recovery disposition and successor-gap routing within its lane; canonical semantic authority, bounded implementation qualification, declared human observation/acceptance and other peer authorities remain separate.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in the Anchor capacity for a bounded context under supplied controlling artifacts and an explicit holder-role binding when Tooling requires one

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, process or runtime currently holds Anchor; that a Handoff was accepted; that a forecast is a promise; that a Major is complete; that a recoverable carrier is accepted or release-ready; that organizational work provenance is known when no explicit relation can be traversed; that Anchor has authority outside controlling artifacts; or that conversation memory is durable project truth.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, model-personality prompt, permanent project ownership, universal semantic authority, schema-governance supremacy, implementation ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, product acceptance, a required Major schema, a fixed Major-numbering algorithm, semantic merge authority, or authority to infer project/org-specific names in generic Tooling.
