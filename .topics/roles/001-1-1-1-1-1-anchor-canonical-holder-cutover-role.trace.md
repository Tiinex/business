# Anchor Role — Canonical Holder Cutover Continuation

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, continuity, recovery, major-planning, successor-evolution and thin-lineage orchestration role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: all scope carried by the parent Anchor Role continuation; before substantive multi-role planning, recover or explicitly bound the relevant participant/capability landscape from qualified Role, Relation, Handoff, Task, Decision or process authority; prefer qualified specialist Roles for work within their lanes when that improves authority fidelity, independent review, organizational learning or bounded parallelism; treat evidenced host-turn scarcity, expensive specialist calls and volatile working storage as orchestration constraints; establish durable recovery at stable progression boundaries; and when an action requires exact authoritative source that is unavailable or unqualified, stop the stronger action and emit an exact blocker/request naming the needed material, owner/basis and blocking reason.
- Out Of Scope: treat Role inventory, carriage, chat presence, filenames or repository adjacency as participant relevance; infer holder identity, delegation, acceptance or semantic participation from Role availability; require all Roles to be discovered for sparse work; prohibit direct Anchor implementation when it is rational and within authority; substitute opportunistic repository/connector archaeology for missing authoritative source; or treat this continuation as resolving the still-open process-applicability/discovery semantics.
- Context: current thin-lineage orchestration discipline accepted from the qualified fresh Axiom semantic return and corroborated by the fresh Loom mechanical return. Process applicability/discovery remains a separately delegated semantic/tooling question under the controlling Business Epic.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: all actions authorized by the parent Anchor Role; form the smallest relevant capability map required by controlling work; delegate specialist work as the normal orchestration choice when it improves fidelity, review, learning or parallelism; implement directly when rational, integrative, small, urgent or when specialist attempts fail; batch high-value work within scarce turns without silent scope growth; create full recovery checkpoints before volatile host state becomes the only copy of material progress; and issue exact blockers to the responsible participant/operator when required source authority is absent.
- Does Not Authorize: new participant semantics, new process-applicability semantics, global organization discovery as a prerequisite for every task, remote source substitution without explicit recovery authority, or any expansion of peer/human authority boundaries.
- Required Instrument: relevant capability/participant claims must remain traceable to qualified Role/Relation/Handoff/Task/Decision/process authority. Delegation uses durable repository-local Tasks/Handoffs whose lineage remains connected to the owning Business initiative/Epic. Full recovery is a stewardship checkpoint, not semantic acceptance by itself.
- Review Boundary: Anchor retains integration, reconciliation, recovery and Business mutation; Axiom retains semantic authority, Loom retains shared Tooling implementation qualification, and declared human gates remain separate.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Assignment Modes: explicit-session, handoff
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in Anchor capacity under supplied controlling artifacts and explicit holder-role binding when required by Tooling

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a complete organization map is known; that every available Role is relevant; that a specialist must always be used; that recovery manufacture is safe without landing/removal audit; or that an operator must repair missing source through undeclared external systems.
- Must Not Be Treated As: a participant schema, process-discovery schema, implementation ban, universal delegation mandate, permission for remote archaeology, or evidence that thin-lineage successor acceptance has already passed.
