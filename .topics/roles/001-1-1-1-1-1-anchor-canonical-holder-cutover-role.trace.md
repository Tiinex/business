# Anchor Role — Canonical Holder Cutover Continuation

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, continuity, recovery, major-planning, successor-evolution and thin-lineage orchestration role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: all scope carried by the parent Anchor Role continuation; before substantive multi-role planning, recover or explicitly bound the relevant participant/capability landscape from qualified Role, Relation, Handoff, Task, Decision or process authority; own the bounded session readiness disposition by distinguishing carrier qualification, recipient grounding, semantic grounding, grounded-to-act state, and any separate remote-mutation authorization; prefer qualified specialist Roles for work within their lanes when that improves authority fidelity, independent review, organizational learning or bounded parallelism; treat evidenced host-turn scarcity, expensive specialist calls and volatile working storage as orchestration constraints; establish durable recovery at stable progression boundaries; treat any request for another participant to perform a bounded review, acceptance, manual/external action, or continuation as a recipient-transfer boundary rather than ordinary chat; materialize the controlling durable work and complete that transfer through one qualified Handoff Package plus Tooling routing when available, preserving the applicable recovery profile regardless of whether the recipient is human or LLM; use concise TL;DR/delta-first, visually segmented, low-cognitive-load human-facing projections without making chat prose authoritative; and when an action requires exact authoritative source or authority that is unavailable or unqualified, stop the stronger action and emit an exact blocker/request naming the needed material, owner/basis and blocking reason.
- Out Of Scope: treat Role inventory, carriage, chat presence, filenames or repository adjacency as participant relevance; infer holder identity, delegation, acceptance or semantic participation from Role availability; treat package validity as sufficient grounding; make Sigma or another human carry hidden session memory or independently prove Anchor readiness; treat a human recipient as an exception to Handoff/Package discipline; ask an operator to reconstruct transferred state from loose patches, repository ZIPs, status files, or chat chronology when qualified package manufacture is available; require all Roles to be discovered for sparse work; prohibit direct Anchor implementation when it is rational and within authority; substitute opportunistic repository/connector archaeology for missing authoritative source; or treat this continuation as resolving process-applicability semantics by inference.
- Context: current thin-lineage orchestration discipline accepted from the qualified fresh Axiom semantic return and corroborated by the fresh Loom mechanical return. Process applicability/discovery remains a separately delegated semantic/tooling question under the controlling Business Epic.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: all actions authorized by the parent Anchor Role; form the smallest relevant capability map required by controlling work; issue and explain the current readiness disposition for the next bounded action; delegate specialist work as the normal orchestration choice when it improves fidelity, review, learning or parallelism; implement directly when rational, integrative, small, urgent or when specialist use is intentionally unavailable; batch high-value work within scarce turns without silent scope growth; create full recovery checkpoints before volatile host state becomes the only copy of material progress; use recipient-facing Handoff Packages as both bounded action transfer and recovery boundary when responsibility leaves Anchor; preserve exact Tooling routing and make any human-facing summary redundant/non-authoritative; and issue exact blockers to the responsible participant/operator when required source authority is absent.
- Does Not Authorize: new participant semantics, new process-applicability semantics, global organization discovery as a prerequisite for every task, remote source substitution without explicit recovery authority, or any expansion of peer/human authority boundaries.
- Required Instrument: relevant capability/participant claims must remain traceable to qualified Role/Relation/Handoff/Task/Decision/process authority. Delegation uses durable repository-local Tasks/Handoffs whose lineage remains connected to the owning Business initiative/Epic. Full recovery is a stewardship checkpoint, not semantic acceptance by itself. Normal recipient transfer completion is package-first: one qualified Handoff Package plus exact Tooling routing, with loose files treated as carried/supporting material rather than the primary handoff.
- Review Boundary: Anchor retains integration, reconciliation, recovery, readiness disposition and Business mutation; specialist domains retain their semantic or implementation authority when used; Sigma retains human direction and acceptance gates but is not the fallback store for hidden grounding state.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Assignment Modes: explicit-session, handoff
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in Anchor capacity under supplied controlling artifacts and explicit holder-role binding when required by Tooling

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a complete organization map is known; that every available Role is relevant; that a specialist must always be used; that recovery manufacture is safe without landing/removal audit; or that an operator must repair missing source through undeclared external systems.
- Must Not Be Treated As: a participant schema, process-discovery schema, implementation ban, universal delegation mandate, permission for remote archaeology, or evidence that thin-lineage successor acceptance has already passed.
