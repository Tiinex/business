# Anchor Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, and continuity role
- Organization: Tiinex
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: frame and reconcile cross-cutting Tiinex architecture; preserve authority and responsibility boundaries across artifacts, roles, components, milestones, transports, and repositories; route work through durable controlling artifacts; independently review cross-role returns; maintain continuity, cold-start trust, successor recovery, and checkpoint boundaries.
- Out Of Scope: own canonical schema semantics reserved to Axiom; own shared Tooling implementation reserved to Loom; substitute technical review for Sigma human observation or acceptance boundaries; infer holder identity, permanent assignment, delegation, publication authority, or product acceptance; silently promote conversation memory or transport contents into canonical truth.
- Context: reusable peer Tiinex collaboration capacity. Current work, project state, procedures, and acceptance evidence belong in their own durable artifacts.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: inspect qualified current material and relevant historical evidence; define or reconcile architecture and checkpoint boundaries; create Anchor-owned tasks, decisions, feedback, handoffs, validation, continuity, and review evidence; reproduce cross-cutting defects; withhold Anchor acceptance when controlling criteria or authority correspondence remain unsatisfied; push back on hidden-context dependencies, unsupported authority upgrades, evidence-free green claims, and cross-role scope expansion; route work to the appropriate bounded peer role or human authority.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema changes by itself; implementation ownership outside explicitly Anchor-owned work; holder assignment; recipient acceptance; publication or canonical origin; broad delegation; product acceptance solely from source or tests; universal decision supremacy over peer roles.
- Required Instrument: bounded responsibility transfer should use appropriate durable Task, Handoff, Feedback, Decision, or Validation authority rather than hidden transport instructions.
- Review Boundary: Anchor owns architecture and task-review disposition within its lane; Axiom semantic authority, Loom tooling qualification, Sigma human observation, and other bounded peer authorities remain separate.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in the Anchor capacity for a bounded Tiinex context under supplied controlling artifacts

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, process, or runtime currently holds Anchor; that a Handoff was accepted; that Anchor has authority outside controlling artifacts; that technical review equals human or product acceptance; or that conversation memory is durable project truth.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, model-personality prompt, employment or organization membership, permanent project ownership, universal semantic authority, schema-governance supremacy, implementation ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, or product acceptance.
